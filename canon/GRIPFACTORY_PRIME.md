## 0. What this node is

Gripfactory-prime is the future fully connected project: Gripfactory Lite's lessons carried into the connected build. What "fully connected" names (METRC by API, Microsoft 365, Zoho, the sensor bridge, the lab portals, whatever the discussion adds), who receives it, where it is hosted, where its data lives, and whether it is Lite grown up or a successor beside Lite: all of it waits on the scoping discussion. This file exists so that ideas said today have a place to wait, verbatim, until that discussion.

The rules of reading are Lite's: nothing is canon until it appears in this file; names here are exact; changes land through the Loop (decide, ratify, fold, version bump). Until the project has its own folder, the Lite Scribe folds this file and mirrors it to the gripfactoryPractical project docs.

## 1. The founding ruling (owner, 2026-09-23, verbatim)

"Start a new canon node for "gripfactory-prime": consider a hideable left menu for all the items on the top bar and the admin and reports button.  This will have to be a discussion for the future fully connected project."

## 2. Parked for the discussion

- **P-1 THE HIDEABLE LEFT MENU.** One collapsible left rail carrying everything the Lite app bar carries (Home, Log, the person, EN and ES, the device switch, the Grip and Lite views) and Reports and Admin with them; the top bar shrinks to the brand and a menu glyph, or goes; the working surface takes the whole width when the rail is hidden. Open for the discussion: what stays visible with the rail hidden (the brand, the glyph, the back crumb); whether the rail remembers its state per device or per person; the phone form (the rail as a drawer over the page); the Admin PIN gate, which does not move; whether the rail lists the stations of the pipeline registry (`app/src/pipeline.ts`) so the menu is data as the stations are.
- **P-2 THE LAYOUT PRECEDENT FROM LITE** (owner, 2026-09-23, on the Prepack scheduling page: "I'd like them to be anchored to the sides of the screen and still be equal to each other" and "make "reports" and "admin" always on top of the screen"). Three equal panes anchored to the sides of the screen, the third free to extend as far as it needs; Reports and Admin on top, never in a space of their own at the bottom. Lite builds this as its own slice (Lite canon 0.30.201); prime inherits the principle, and P-1 is where it goes next.

## 3. Open questions for the scoping discussion

What "fully connected" names, connection by connection, and which of them Lite already has in a lesser form (the METRC Mirror by export, the Import Data uploads, the Graph pull that waits on the Entra ids). Whether prime is one codebase with Lite (the same Worker and D1 with the connections lit) or a successor that reads Lite's canon as upstream and starts clean on the same registry. Where the data lives and who owns the account. Who receives it (Grip internal, the Continuum 10, both). When the node earns its own folder under `Documents\Claude\Projects`, its own repo with no remotes, and its own Scribe, and what of this file moves with it.
