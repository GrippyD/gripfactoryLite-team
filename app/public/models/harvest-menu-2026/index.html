Base price (mean) $ / lb

Hang driedSilo dried

Base = the standard (mean-potency) price for each environment × dry method. Under each: the −1σ · +1σ prices at that grow type's spread, plus the category's mean THC (μ) and σ. The spread slider under each grow type moves lots off base by their σ distance from the category mean — 0% puts every lot at base, 100% puts a +2σ lot at 2× base (floor 25%). Lots with no THC estimate stay at base. Starter prices are placeholders. A strain-level override (in Edit) beats the matrix.
