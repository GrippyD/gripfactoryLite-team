## What landed

The entry URL is the check-in. The POST fields stay `cell:<kpi_key>|<dim_key>` and `reason`. The save, the log, and the refusals of a bad number are the ones S2a built. A current period with nothing saved yet sends the hidden reason "Weekly check-in". A past period, or a save that would replace a hand value, shows "You are changing a saved number. Say why." and requires it. After a save the redirect still carries `ok=saved&n=` and adds `#dd-next` so the page opens at the first empty card.

The KPI page leads with how often, the unit, the goal in words, the season, and whether you type it, the app fills it, or both. Goal kind, goal ref, compute key, status, definition version, config key, and dims sit in a closed "Definition details" section. History is period, value (Total, then farms), goal, and on goal or off goal.

The target form is "Set a goal": the number, "Goal for each" with the row's own kind selected, and Starting on the current period. "Spread a yearly goal" (Evenly or Custom) is closed and is omitted on a day, quarter, or season. "Range (optional)" is closed. "Which farm (optional)" shows only when the row has farms. A second live goal on the same start date now says "A goal for that week already starts on that date." The stored row is still S2b's.

Questions are closed cards: the question, the current answer, ruled or open, and Change. Open questions come first, then ruled. The positions, the recommendation, and the affected KPIs are inside the card.
