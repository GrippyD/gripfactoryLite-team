Cross cutting facts that change the build (all with file and line in the registry rows):

1. No week exists in the code. Nothing groups by week; the only roll ups are hourly (sensor_hourly) and monthly (gc_actual_monthly). Every weekly tile needs a week law minted in S1 (debate week_definition) and tested at the edges in both time zones.
2. Two harvest years and seven day definitions. The console's harvest year is the calendar year of the farm today, lots carry harvest_year = calendar year of the HBN entry date (src/mint.ts:60-62), and GRIPCasting runs April to March from the close date (src/gripcasting/model.ts:132-137). The farm day (6 AM Eastern, farmday.ts) governs the console; Equipment, the lab Dashboard, TATs and the Product Mix default read UTC days. HTD must name one start (debate htd_start) and every snapshot must bucket on the farm day, never a raw UTC date.
3. The lot date law is unsettled. The console's day pane sums wet_weights.created_at inside farm day bounds with no toll gate (src/console.ts:2850-2858); the year pane gates on harvest_year and own lots; the reconciliation report windows on harvest_batches.entry_date; two helpers already fall back from effective_date differently (src/csv.ts:355-361 to farmDay(created_at); src/plantcounts.ts:100 to entry_date). Every "yesterday" figure waits on this ruling (debate lot_date_law).
4. Four per plant lot sets and four BTR pools. Per plant: the console (status not weighing, plant_count at least 1), strain performance (unloaded with a count, and a lot with no dry grams counts as zero), puzzles (every lot, toll and history included), dry weight by strain (unload date range). - Licence: the terms list "integrating our service into commercial products" as commercial use, so a grower's operational tool should assume API Standard (USD 29 a month, 1M calls) or Professional (USD 99, adds the archive); free tier limits are 10,000 calls a day, 5,000 an hour, 600 a minute; CC BY 4.0 attribution with a link next to the display.
- Conversion: DLI (mol per square metre per day) = shortwave_radiation_sum x 2.04, from the 1984 Agronomy Journal study (daily PPFD 2.04 plus or minus 0.06 times solar irradiance, PAR about 45 percent of global), cross checked by 0.45 x 4.57 micromol per joule = 2.06; published spreads run 1.7 to 2.3 by season and sky, so the tile carries a plus or minus 10 to 15 percent caveat and the word estimate. Worked: 14.62 MJ gives 29.8 mol; 6.12 MJ gives 12.5 mol. The forecast and archive feeds snap to different grid cells and differ by up to about 2 MJ on the same day, so the source rides every row and the two are never merged.
- Fit: one GET per farm per day on the 15 past cron, keyed (farm_code, day), recording only completed local days; three farms is trivial on the paid plan.
- Owner hands: latitude and longitude per farm (the farms table holds only code and name; a migration adds the columns), the licence tier and subscription with OPEN_METEO_API_KEY set by hand, the attribution link, the factor policy (fixed 2.04 or seasonal) and whether to backfill past seasons.

### Michigan CRA public data (market penetration denominator): needs owner, hand entry recommended

- Source: the CRA Monthly Statistical Report (a .docx per month since March 2026, PDF before; URL pattern {Month}-{YYYY}-Monthly-Report.docx, two 2025 exceptions) is the only primary source that states active licence counts by type. September 2026: 837 active adult use Retailer licences (August 845), 86 medical Provisioning Centers, 321 home delivery approved retailers, adult use product sales by category totalling USD 242,263,191.56, average retail flower price USD 59.99 an ounce. Published about two to three weeks after month end. No CSV or xlsx of licensees exists at a stable URL; the per licensee list comes only from a blank search plus Download results in the Accela portal (a WebForms postback chain).
- Blockers for an unattended fetch: the michigan.gov Terms of Use forbid automated access and commercial use of the data without a written agreement, and the Akamai edge returns 403 to non browser user agents (a browser user agent string clears it, which is spoofing a deliberate control). Labels repeat inside the document (medical section first, adult use second), so a parser must anchor on section order and fail loudly.
- Recommendation: type the figures by hand once a month from the browser (retailer count, provisioning centers, sales by category, flower price) with the report month and URL; the only automation is an in app reminder around the 20th. Build the docx parser only if the owner obtains written permission from the State.
- Owner hands: the denominator definition (Retailer only, plus microbusinesses, plus provisioning centers; co located licences count twice in the report), hand entry or a written agreement, and no personal names from the report covers in D1.
