## The objects

A click on a tool arms it. The next click on the label drops the object. The line takes two clicks, and each end is an anchor that moves freely.

- Text Box: one line. Font from Arial, Times New Roman, Courier New, Georgia, Verdana. Size, bold, italic, alignment.
- Memo: several lines. The type picker is Text, Number, or Alphanumeric, and it only governs what may be typed. Fonts as Text Box.
- Barcode: Code 128 through `code128.ts`. Toggle caption centers the data beneath the bars, and the font scales with the box.
- QR: through `qr.ts`. Toggle caption the same.
- Picture: a JPEG, PNG, or BMP from the device, stored inside the template. Over 1 MB is refused in words. A file that is not one of those three is refused in words.
- Shapes: rectangle, ellipse, rounded rectangle, triangle. Outline thickness, fill none or black.
- Line: thickness, and dashes solid, dashed, or dotted.

A click selects an object (an outline and its anchors). Hovering it shows the four-way move cursor. Press and drag moves it, snapping to the eighth inch. Shift moves freely. Boxes, codes, pictures, and shapes have a corner resize anchor. A line has an anchor on each end. Rotate, in the options pane, is 90 degree steps for codes and pictures and any whole degree for text, memo, and shapes. Delete and Backspace remove the selected object. Arrow keys nudge it an eighth inch. The options pane shows the selected object's options, or the armed tool's.

Text Box, Memo, Barcode, and QR carry Attach: a picker of the label's other objects that hold data. The attached object shows that object's content, through one hop or two. A text box attached to a barcode shows the barcode's data. A barcode attached to a text box encodes the text. A cycle is refused in words.

Source Data (on a text box) and Connections (in the tool column) show and answer "Coming in the next slice".
