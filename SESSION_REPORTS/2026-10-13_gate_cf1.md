# Gate CF1: the cure pane count over the conditions line (sealed 2026-10-13)

The owner, with a picture of the Harvest Console's CSA and CSB panes: "all the text in these two panes runs together when they're full, how do we fix?" Built by the Scribe in branches/GFLiteMetrcAPI (free at main 20413c1), one file: src/cure.ts (27,484 bytes, 70be7d86c814e40a). Tree e57aea288fdafeda on the pin e7bd19cbcad90250.

## The cause
When a pane is nearly full the green shade reaches the header, and a small script pads the shade so its count (94% full, 34 Lots) starts below the header. The script added up the heights of the room name and the conditions line but left out the tile's top padding and the margins, so it came up about 20 px short. When the conditions line wraps to two lines (temperature, humidity, units, minutes ago, Silo Ops), the count rode over its second line. Measured on the live-data bench: conditions end at 83 px, count started at 64 px.

## The fix
The script now measures where the conditions line really ends inside the pane (its bottom edge plus its margin) and pads the shade to clear it. It measures again on load, on resize, when fonts settle, when any fold opens (the console's Drying section is folded), and whenever a pane changes size. No style, no page, and no figure changes.
