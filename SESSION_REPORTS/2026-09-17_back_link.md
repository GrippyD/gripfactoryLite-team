## The law, as built

1. THE BACK LINK. Every page in the app shell but Home carries one crumb, first thing under the app bar: a left arrow and the name of the menu above it (`<nav class="back-crumb"><a class="back-link" href="..." data-back-link>&#8592; Menu</a></nav>`), in the crew's language. Home is the top of the tree and carries none.
2. The tree is data. The operations map (pipeline.ts OPS_GROUPS) places the stations and the group pages: a group page (/ops/harvest_ops, /ops/drying_ops, /ops/production_ops, /ops/business_ops) and a group whose own page is its member (Interco Logistics, Marketplace) go back to Home; every member goes back to its group (Acquire HBN and Wet Weight Entry to Harvest Operations; Load Silo, Unload Dry Space and Virtual move to Drying Operations; Production Scheduling, Trim Packages Entry, Cryo Weight Entry, Fulfillment and Inventory to Production Operations; Compliance Console and Purchasing to Business Operations). A prefix table (nav.ts) places the families: the scheduling stages to Production Scheduling; Fulfillment's, Inventory's, Marketplace's, Compliance's and Purchasing's pages to their landings (an order page to the Marketplace's Orders pane); Admin to Home and its pages to Admin; the lookup sections to Data Lookups; the upload files to Pending Data Uploads; /queue and the movement void to Data Queues; Harvest Console and Models to Admin, a model tool to Models; Reports to Home and each report to Reports; the change log to Home; anything unplaced to Home rather than nowhere.
3. A record's pencil or void page goes back to the page it came from (the same-origin referer, never another record page) and reads Back; with no referer it goes to Data Queues.
4. The pages that carried their own back line under the title (Production Scheduling's landing and stages, Load Silo, the Data Lookups sections, Manual HBN, each report, the reserved Inventory pages, the Compliance and Labels pages, Purchasing's pages, Fulfillment's Mount SOs and Pick Orders, a Marketplace order, the group pages' Operations link) repeat no link of their own now; the crumb is the one way back. Links that did more than go back stay (the PO's print button, Pick Orders' Mount SOs).
5. THE FULL PATH RULE (standing, his word): every PowerShell block the Scribe hands him begins with the full path of the folder it runs in, `cd "C:\Users\DennisWallace\Documents\Claude\Projects\gripfactory Practical\app"` for the app.
