## What changed

The Trimming section opens with a Right now pane (`data-hc-trim-rightnow`) the width of the section. Its head reads Right now, the farm's today, and the shift on duty (Day shift, Second Shift, or "no shift on duty" in a gap; Day wins when both windows hold). Under that is one machine picture of today's live line: every package weighed today at the trim node, both shifts and any operator on neither, plus today's finished lots and their WIP left. The picture fills the pane. T-0's typed input pounds are not drawn on it. Under the picture the line total is labeled Line total. A full-width BTR bar (`data-hc-btr-bar`, `btrBar`) sits under the picture: bud green `#2e9e4f` for today's live BTR, consumed grey `#b5bab3` for the rest, the percent at the end ("BTR 61.2%"), and the bogey as a tick with its percent under the tick. Nothing run today is an empty outline reading "no packages today".

The pane reads RUNNING (`.hc-running`) when any trim-node package was weighed in the last 30 minutes, or a shift on duty has a lot in the Unloaded Queue (canon 0.30.163, both shifts together). Otherwise it reads DOWN (`data-hc-trim-down`, bold, danger color) with a clock from the last package weighed at the trim node, any day (`data-clock`, `data-state="running"`, `data-started` at that instant's ms, first text from `formatClock`). If nothing was ever weighed, DOWN has no timer. The flow bars move while RUNNING and stand still while DOWN.

The two shift cards keep the day picked, their operators, their own RUNNING flag, the centered bud (waffle, BTR read, legend), and the metrics: Other, Bulk Bud, Prepack Bud, Total Bud, BTR, Lbs Produced Yesterday, DLH Yesterday with its hours box, Lbs/DLH, and the input lbs box. They no longer carry the machine picture or the sheet hours line. Under the cards, `data-hc-trim-totals` reads Total Lbs (every package of the day, both shifts and unassigned, pounds with grams under), Total Hours (the typed DLH summed, the one typed value when only one shift has hours, "type hours" when neither), and Lbs/DLH (nothing without hours). The unassigned grams note and the unassigned RUNNING note stay under that line.

Harvest hours are renamed Harvest Hours Yesterday (EN and ES, including the aria label). The three farm boxes keep Weight Harvested and lose the hours box and Weight/DLH. The Total box has the one hours box, posting `farm_code = 'ALL'` through `saveHarvestHours` (insert, update, clear, change log, the same as a farm). The box shows the ALL row when one stands, otherwise the sum of the farm rows for that day. Weight/DLH on Total is the day's total wet weight over that figure. The sheet sync status line (`data-hc-sheet-sync-line`) sits under the Total hours. Sheet sync itself still writes per farm and never writes ALL. The labor hours report names a farm row "Harvest hours, <farm>" and the ALL row "Harvest hours".
