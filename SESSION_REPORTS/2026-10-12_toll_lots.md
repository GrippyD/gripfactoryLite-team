## What was built

Migration 0099 adds `harvest_batches.customer TEXT`. It writes no row. NULL is Grip's own lot. A lot with a customer is a toll lot: another company's material that the floor works.

The manual HBN form gains "Customer (toll lot)", blank by default, beside the test box. The batch Edit form shows the same field. No other door sets it. A test and a customer together are refused in words: "A lot cannot be both a test and a toll lot." A toll lot takes the next serial in the line (`is_test = 0`, the sequencer 0095 already counts it). A test still takes a serial from 90001 and holds no place.

Grip's own figures use one fragment, `ownLotSql` in `src/toll.ts` (`OWN_LOT_SQL` is that fragment on the `b` alias). It keeps a plain lot and leaves out a test and a toll lot. The season queries in `console.ts`, Assign Plant Counts and dry per plant in `plantcounts.ts`, and the Grip rows of harvest weight reconciliation use it. The day's wet weight, the queues, trim, cryo, and the harvest batch feed still show a toll lot. The feed already names `customer` once the column exists, and a toll lot rides because it is not a test.

On an HBN cell, the customer sits where Test sits today (`GreenCo-Toll-26111 GreenCo`, `data-hbn-customer`).

Dry Weight by Strain leaves a toll lot out of the strain rows and the Total, and prints `Toll lots: {customer} {n} lots, {g} g dry` under that Total. The CSV carries the line as its own row. Harvests by year is the season report here: toll batches stay out of the year totals, and the same line is a row under the year rows. Floor vs METRC keeps toll packages and toll lots in a Toll lots section. Harvest weight reconciliation does the same, and those lots are not in the wet total or the dry total.
