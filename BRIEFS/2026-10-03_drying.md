The two `app_config` keys stay in the table, unread after this slice (say so in the report). The fullness stays a count of lots over the cap (his pick).
3. MEMBERSHIP STAYS AUTOMATIC. Which lots a cure space holds is `cureMembership` exactly as today; nothing new is recorded on the floor. The panes find their rooms by the space name (`CSA`, `CSB`); the sensor door's rooms and the Silo Ops push are unchanged.
4. THE ROWS. Wherever the strip draws, it draws one row per kind of space, in this order: Silo, Freezer, Cure Space, Dry Room. Silo: the space_type `silo` spaces. Freezer: the space_type `freezer` spaces, then the FFZ inventory cube. Cure Space: CSA then CSB (V28's tiles, bar and count as they are). Dry Room: the space_type `dry_room` spaces on the strip today (the Arlington Dry Room). A row with no tile is not drawn. Each row holds its tiles in one line; past five tiles the row scrolls sideways with an arrow at each end that shows only when there is more to see (the five silos fit without one). Tile width stays 13.6rem.

The three-figure drying totals line under the strip stays as it is.
6. THE TILE COLLAPSES. Every tile on every row (silos, the freezer, the FFZ cube, the cure spaces, the dry rooms) is collapsed by default on each page load: its name, a small icon of its shape (the silo's cylinder, the freezer's and the FFZ cube's cube, the dry room's box, the cure space's bar in its fill), its status (the silo's state word; the freezer's status and percent; the FFZ cube's figure; the cure space's "{pct}% full - {n} Lots"; the dry room's state word) and its weight (wet for silos, the freezer and dry rooms; dry for the cure spaces). A click or tap on the tile's head opens it to the tile as it draws today, and again closes it. Opening a cure space's tile shows its V28 bar folded; its count still opens the lot list as V28 built. A collapsed tile keeps its colors and its throbbing state (loading green, running red, the freezer's blue) on the icon. Keyboard: the head is a button with `aria-expanded`.
7. THE LIVE TILES STAY LIVE. `siloLiveScript` keeps every tile current, collapsed or open, in its row; the clocks keep ticking. `/silos/live.json` keeps its shape (anything added is additive; Silo Ops reads it).
