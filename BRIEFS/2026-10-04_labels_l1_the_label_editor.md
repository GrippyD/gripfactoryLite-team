## The law

1. THE LABELS MENU. `/compliance/labels` (the Compliance tile keeps its link) opens on three buttons: Quick Print, Label Editor, Custom Label Data. The current view (the template form, the package list, `/compliance/labels/print`) is removed; `label_templates` keeps its rows. Quick Print and Custom Label Data answer "Coming in the next slices" until L2 and L3 land.
2. THE EDITOR, `/compliance/labels/editor`, laid out as the drawing: the tool column at the left (the buttons in the drawing's order and pairs, the options pane, Exit back to the menu); the work area at the right with the top and left rulers in inches (major ticks each inch, minor each eighth, numbered), the label-size button where they meet, and the grid (one cell each quarter inch). The grid outside the label is the app's panel color; the label area is white ("The grid area should turn white in the label area"). Desktop and tablet widths; the work area scrolls when the label is larger than the screen.
3. THE LABEL SIZE. The corner button opens a small panel: Length and Width in inches (0.5 to 12, to the hundredth), the stock presets of Admin's `label_stock` lookup (4 x 6, 2 x 1) as one-tap fills, Apply. The label's orientation follows the numbers (L across, W down; say which word is which on the panel, EN and ES).
4. THE OBJECTS. A click on a tool arms it; the next click on the label drops the object there (the line takes two clicks). Text Box (one line; font family from a short list of print-safe fonts, size, bold, italic, alignment), Memo (several lines; the type picker Text, Number, Alphanumeric, which only governs what may be typed into it; fonts as Text Box), Barcode (Code128 through the app's `code128.ts`; Toggle caption: the data centered beneath, its font scaling with the box), QR (through the app's `qr.ts`; Toggle caption the same), Picture (a JPEG, PNG or BMP chosen from the device, stored inside the template, at most 1 MB each, refused larger in words), Shapes (rectangle, ellipse, rounded rectangle, triangle; outline thickness and fill none or black), Line (two clicks; each end an anchor that moves freely; Thickness; Dashes solid, dashed, dotted).
5. SELECT, MOVE, RESIZE, ROTATE. A click selects an object (an outline and its anchors); hovering it shows the four-way move cursor; press and drag moves it, snapping to the eighth inch with Shift to move freely. Every object has at least one resize anchor (a corner anchor on boxes, codes, pictures and shapes; end anchors on a line); Rotate in the options pane in 90 degree steps for codes and pictures and any whole degree for text, memo and shapes. Delete and Backspace remove the selected object; arrow keys nudge it an eighth inch. The options pane always shows the selected object's options, or the armed tool's.
6. ATTACH. Text Box, Memo, Barcode and QR carry Attach: a picker of the label's other objects that hold data; the attached object shows that object's content (a text box attached to a barcode shows its data; a barcode attached to a text box encodes the text). Cycles are refused. Source Data and Connections are L2's (their buttons show and answer "Coming in the next slice").
7. TEMPLATES. Save Template stores what is on screen under a name (new, or over an existing one after a confirm); Open Template lists the saved templates (name, size, last saved, by whom) and loads one; New warns, offers to save, then clears every object. Migration 0092 adds to `label_templates`: `layout_json` (the objects and their properties, versioned with a `v: 1`), `length_in` and `width_in`; `width_mm`, `height_mm` and `fields_json` are still written (the inches converted, `fields_json` `{"fields":[]}`) so nothing that reads them breaks. Every save logs to `record_change_log` under the actor.
8. PRINT (this slice): prints the label on screen once through the browser's print dialog with the page the label's size (`@page { size: Lin Win; margin: 0 }`), every object placed in inches as on screen, barcodes and QR as vector, text as text. L3 brings the modal Quick Print.
9. THE ICONS (his words above). Every tool button's icon is drawn for this slice as inline SVG in the app's line style (one stroke weight, the view's accent, rounded joins, legible at 28 px and 20 px), not emoji, not borrowed artwork; one icon per tool in the drawing's meaning (folder, disk, blank page, printer, plug or link for Connections, the text cursor, a note, bars, a QR finder pattern, a picture frame, the four shapes, a line with end dots, a door for Exit). Render them in Lite, CO and Cerberus and attach a sheet of all of them at both sizes to the report.
10. WORDS: EN and ES for every label, button, option, warning and refusal.
