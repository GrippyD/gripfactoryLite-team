## The law to build

1. THE TABLE, migration `0081_fulfillment_override.sql`: `fulfillment_overrides (id TEXT PRIMARY KEY, entry_date TEXT NOT NULL, row_key TEXT NOT NULL, col TEXT NOT NULL, value NUMERIC NOT NULL, unit TEXT NOT NULL, created_at, created_by, updated_at, updated_by, deleted_at, deleted_by, delete_reason)`, `entry_date` a calendar day `YYYY-MM-DD` (the farm day), `col` one of `accepted`, `submitted`, `shipping_today`, `unit` `g` or `ea`; a partial unique index on `(entry_date, row_key, col) WHERE deleted_at IS NULL` and an index on `(entry_date)`. One `app_config` row seeded with `INSERT OR IGNORE`: `fulfillment_unit_grams` = `prepack=28, mobs=70, prerolls=1, vapes=1`, the grams in one each for the rows METRC counts as eaches (the bar's proportions; the assumption below).
2. THE ROWS, one list in the new module, in the doc's order, each with its key, its name and its METRC unit: `bulk_bud` Bulk Bud (g); `biomass_trim` Biomass/Trim (g); `prepack` Prepack (ea); `prerolls` Prerolls (ea); `shake` Shake (g); `bulk_distillate` Bulk Distillate (g); `vapes` Vapes (ea); `mobs` MOBs (ea); `bulk_isolate` Bulk Isolate (THCA) (g); `bulk_infused` Bulk Infused (g). The names are the doc's descriptive names (ruling 10). The columns, in order: Accepted, Submitted, Shipping Today.
3. THE ADMIN PAGE, `/admin/lookups/fulfillment_override`, "Fulfillment Override", under Logistics on the lookups landing: a date field that loads today (the farm's today, `farmToday`) and reloads the page for any day picked (`?date=`); the grid, ten rows by three columns, every cell an input masked to its row's unit (a whole number of grams or a whole number of eaches, `min="0" step="1" inputmode="numeric"`, the unit word beside the cell, `data-fulfill-cell="<row>:<col>"`), the row's name at its left, the column heads above; one Save. The save writes the day's cells: a typed cell inserts or updates its row (the unit from the list), a cell left blank where a value stood soft-deletes it (reason "cleared"), a changed value logs old and new in `record_change_log` (table `fulfillment_overrides`, field `value`), an untouched cell writes nothing. Nothing is ever hard-deleted, so the entries of every day are kept for posterity (ruling 23), and the page reloads any past day and shows what was typed. A value that is not a whole number of zero or more refuses in words.
4. THE SECTION, "Fulfillment", `data-hc-section="fulfillment"`, after Prepack, open on a first visit like the others, three panes side by side (the pattern of the gate's four): Shipping today, Submitted, Accepted, each headed by its name. It follows the day picked (ruling 23): the day read is `input.yesterday` as the other sections read it, so today shows today's entries when today is picked. Each pane reads its column's cells for that day, the series present being the rows whose value for the day is not null (the doc's law); a series with a value of zero is present and draws a segment of no width, named in the legend with its zero.
5. THE BARS. Shipping today: the stacked bar is drawn inside the trailer, as rects filling `TRAILER_CARGO` left to right, each segment's width the series' share of the pane's day total in grams (a `g` row its value; an `ea` row its value times its unit grams from `fulfillment_unit_grams`), the rects carrying `data-fulfill-segment="<row>"` and a hue class; the icon's own stroke untouched. Submitted and Accepted: the same stacked bar as a horizontal band to the right of the icon (an inline SVG beside it, the bar's height that of the icon's middle third, its width the pane's), the same segments and hues. Ten fixed hues, `.hc-fill-1` to `.hc-fill-10`, in the three sheets (the pie's eight and two more), one per row in the doc's order, so a series wears the same color on every pane and every day. Under each bar a legend: the series present in the doc's order, each with its swatch, its name and its value in its own unit ("Prepack 40 ea", "Bulk Bud 12,000 g"), no total across the mixed units. A pane whose day has no cell at all reads "No figures typed for this day." with the trailer empty and the band absent. The pure functions are `fulfillmentSeries(cells, unitGrams)` (the present series with their grams and shares) and `stackedBar(...)` (the rects), tested.
6. THE LOADER, `loadFulfillmentSection(db, day)`: the day's cells and the config row, one query each; nothing else. Ruling 10: no other source, no fallback.
7. NOTHING ELSE MOVES. `src/fulfillment.ts` (the Zoho lane) is untouched; the console's other sections, the day control, the fold script, the icons module untouched; `scripts/smoke.mjs` moves its pin to 81 and walks the page: the landing button, the grid with today loaded, a POST with Bulk Bud 1000 g and Prepack 40 ea in Shipping Today and Vapes left blank, the console with today picked showing the Shipping today pane with two segments (`data-fulfill-segment="bulk_bud"`, `data-fulfill-segment="prepack"`) and no vapes segment, the Submitted and Accepted panes reading the empty words, then a second POST changing Prepack to 50 and a change-log row with old 40 and new 50, then the grid for that day reloading 50.

## Assumptions for his word

- The units per row follow METRC's own: grams for Bulk Bud, Biomass/Trim, Shake, Bulk Distillate, Bulk Isolate (THCA) and Bulk Infused; eaches for Prepack, Prerolls, Vapes and MOBs. The Zoho lane's display units (lb, L) are that lane's, not this panel's.
- A stacked bar across mixed units needs one measure: grams, with `fulfillment_unit_grams` giving the grams in one each (Prepack 28 and MOBs 70 from ruling 22; Prerolls 1 and Vapes 1 as placeholders for his numbers, editable in Admin's app_config like the gate's).
- The section stands after Prepack, the last on the console.
- "Follows the day picked": the panes read the console's day; yesterday on load, today when today is picked, any day the control allows.
- The legend shows each series in its typed unit; no total is drawn across grams and eaches.
