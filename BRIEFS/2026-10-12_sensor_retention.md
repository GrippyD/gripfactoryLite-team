## Why

`sensor_readings` held 180,636 rows on 2026-10-12 and grows about 7,000 a day (Silo Ops' door: CSA four units and CSB one, about once a minute each; the SensorPush pull every five minutes). HF1 (0.30.436) bounded every read to the two indexes, so the pages are safe; the table is not: the snapshot dump and any future scan grow with it, and D1 resets the database for CPU time past a scan of about 175,000 rows. The owner chose the rollup over a bare purge so the history survives: one row per unit per hour, kept for good, at about one sixtieth the size.

## The law

1. THE HOURLY TABLE. Migration `0100_sensor_hourly.sql` creates `sensor_hourly (id TEXT PRIMARY KEY, room TEXT NOT NULL, sensor_id TEXT, role TEXT, source TEXT, hour_start TEXT NOT NULL, readings INTEGER NOT NULL, temp_min NUMERIC NOT NULL, temp_max NUMERIC NOT NULL, temp_avg NUMERIC NOT NULL, hum_min NUMERIC, hum_max NUMERIC, hum_avg NUMERIC, first_read_at TEXT NOT NULL, last_read_at TEXT NOT NULL, rolled_at TEXT NOT NULL)` with a unique index on `(room, COALESCE(sensor_id, ''), COALESCE(source, ''), hour_start)` written as a plain unique index over `(room, sensor_key, source_key, hour_start)` where `sensor_key` and `source_key` are stored NOT NULL columns holding the coalesced text (D1 does not take expression indexes reliably), and an index on `(room, hour_start)`. `hour_start` is the UTC hour, ISO, minute and second zero. Two app_config seeds in the same migration, each inserted only where the key is absent: `sensor_retention_days` 90 and `sensor_rollup_through` '' (the watermark, the last UTC hour fully rolled). Test rows (`source` 'test') are never rolled up and are purged on the same clock as the rest.
2. THE ROLLUP RUNS ON THE QUARTER-PAST CRON, bounded. A new `src/sensorhistory.ts` exports `rollupSensorHours(db, nowIso)`: it rolls every hour from the watermark forward up to the hour that ended at least two hours before now (so a late resend lands first), at most 24 hours of readings per run, one `INSERT ... SELECT ... GROUP BY room, sensor_key, source_key` per hour through the `(room, read_at)` index with the hour's bounds bound as parameters, guarded by `WHERE NOT EXISTS` on the unique key so a rerun changes nothing, then moves the watermark. The first runs after deploy walk the backlog from the oldest reading (about 180,000 rows, about 25 days of door data, cleared within a day of hourly runs) and nothing else waits on them. The run is appended to the 15-past block after In Testing, in its own try so a failure there never blocks the sheet, the sweep or In Testing, and logs one line (`[sensors] rolled N hours, R readings, through H`). No new cron string.
3. THE PURGE, 90 DAYS, NEVER THE NEWEST. The same run then deletes raw readings older than `sensor_retention_days` whose hour is at or before the watermark (never a reading that has not been rolled), in chunks of 500 by `(room, read_at)` order, at most 20 chunks a run, and never a sensor's newest row nor a room's newest door row (the newest-per-sensor and newest-per-room reads, the stale flag and the SensorPush cursor keep working even for a unit that went quiet months ago). A test row (`source` 'test') is purged by age without a rollup. The count purged rides in the same log line.
4. THE HISTORY READS FROM THE HOURLY TABLE ALONE. A report `environment_history` on the standing report pattern (`/reports/environment_history`, the Reports menu, EN and ES): a room picker (the rooms of `doorRoomNames` plus any room in `sensor_hourly`), From and To dates (the farm's days, default the last seven), one line per unit per hour with readings, min, avg and max temperature and humidity, the room's hourly average beneath when several units stand; read through `(room, hour_start)` with bound dates; CSV on the report's standing CSV control. Nothing reads `sensor_readings` for history. The Environment page's conditions lines and flags are untouched (they read the newest raw rows, inside the retention window by construction).
5. THE SNAPSHOT AND THE DUMP. `scripts/db-snapshot.mjs` dumps every table by name from the schema, so `sensor_hourly` rides with no change; confirm it does in the smoke's snapshot step if the smoke takes one, else by a read of the script.
6. NOTHING ELSE MOVES. The door, the SensorPush pull, the cure panes, the Environment page, the three cron strings, the other two cron blocks and the mirror sweep stay as they are.
