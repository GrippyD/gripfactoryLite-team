## The law

1. THE SHAPE IS ITS BOX. Every shape draws to its box exactly: the rectangle's and rounded rectangle's edges, the ellipse's extremes and the triangle's points touch the box; the outline stroke is drawn inside the box so nothing prints past it. The selection outline is the box. A text box, memo, code or picture's box is its printed area, as today.
2. EIGHT HANDLES. A selected box object (text box, memo, barcode, QR, picture, every shape) shows eight square handles on its box: four corners, four side midpoints, each with its resize cursor, rotation applied (a rotated object's handles sit on its rotated corners and sides). A corner handle resizes both ways with the opposite corner held; a side handle resizes one way with the opposite side held.
3. JOINTS. A triangle keeps its three points as fractions of its box (`pts`), shown as round joint anchors that each drag alone; a resize by the handles scales them. A layout saved before L5 reads its triangle as today's three points, so nothing prints differently until it is edited. Rectangles, rounded rectangles and ellipses show no joint anchors (their eight handles are their anchors). A line keeps its two end anchors.
4. JOINT SNAP, as `farmVertexSnap`: a dragged triangle joint pulls level or upright with a neighbor joint (dashed guide), else onto an exact 90 degree corner; tolerance about 9 screen pixels at any zoom.
5. MOVE AND HANDLE SNAP, as `farmJointLock`: the joints of the object being moved or resized (a box's four corners, a triangle's points, a line's ends) against every other object's joints and the label's four corners: both axes close locks them (a ring); one axis close aligns that axis (a dashed line). Snap to a joint wins over the eighth inch grid; with nothing in reach the grid holds. Alt turns every snap off, as in the farm editor (Shift keeps moving freely of the grid, as today).
6. EVERYWHERE THE SAME. The editor, L1's print, L3's Quick Print and the preview draw the same geometry; L4's ink, fill, formulas and front and back order are unchanged. Arrow keys still nudge an eighth inch; Delete still removes.
7. WORDS: EN and ES for any new words.
