## The law

1. THE MANUAL MINT. A manual HBN is accepted with any fields when (a) it ends in `-YY###` (two digits, three digits) and (b) no HBN with that exact text exists, live or voided. The YY need not match the entry date, and the ### need not be free. Its `lot_serial` is NOT the typed tail: it is the next number in line, `1 + MAX(lot_serial)` of the entry date's harvest year over rows that are not tests, voided included, never below `first_lot_token` (its phantom slot). The auto mint after it takes the next one (26001 auto, a manual mint holds 26002, the next auto is 26003). The lot reads everywhere by its HBN's own last five, as today (`shortLotId`).
2. TEST HBNS. The manual form gains a box "Test HBN (holds no place in line)" (ES "HBN de prueba (no ocupa lugar en la fila)"). Migration 0095 adds `harvest_batches.is_test INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0`. A test HBN is stored with `is_test = 1`, its harvest year from its entry date, and a `lot_serial` from a reserved band, `90001` and up per year (one past the year's highest test serial), so it never moves the line. Tests stay in the HBN Queue and work as lots do; they are left out of the season figures that read the harvest year (the dashboard's Plants, yields, BTR to date, Harvest Hours to date's lots, plant counts left pane) and say "Test" beside their HBN in the queue.
3. THE AUTO MINT counts only rows with `is_test = 0`. It never mints past 999 in a year without saying so: at 999 it refuses with "The year's lot numbers have reached 999." (EN and ES).
