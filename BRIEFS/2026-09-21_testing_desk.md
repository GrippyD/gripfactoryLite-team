## What the research settled (the law leans on it; details and citations in the RESEARCH files)

1. Michigan tests flower and trim/shake for potency, foreign matter, microbials, heavy metals, chemical residue and water activity (0.65 aw), mycotoxins only after an Aspergillus fail, terpenes optional; medical and adult-use limits differ (yeast and mold tenfold). A harvest batch is one strain, at most 50 lb; the sample is at least 0.5% of the batch (labs use increment schedules, 14 g floor); the laboratory collects the sample and both sign the chain of custody; product under test cannot be packaged, transferred or sold.
2. In Metrc the grower creates the test sample package from the lot package (Submit for Testing, sample quantity, a new tag, `UseSameItem`, the Required Lab Test Batch "Raw Plant Material"), the transfer type is "Testing Transfer (to SCF)" and the manifest is made in the Metrc UI (no API for a licensed outgoing transfer in Michigan; templates only); the lab records results and uploads the COA to Metrc within 3 business days; the source package's lab testing state moves through the 14 documented strings (NotSubmitted, SubmittedForTesting, TestingInProgress, TestPassed, TestFailed, RetestPassed, RetestFailed, Remediated, and the rest); a retest must pass two separate new samples consecutively; remediation is by CRA approval and marked in Metrc.
3. TagLeaf has no client API; SC Labs Michigan runs on it (portal calendar for pickups, COA pages at lims.tagleaf.com/coa_/<id>, result emails), ACT Lab runs its own Slingr portal; the TOS bans bots. So the lab is an external system reached by people, email and, once the keys land, Metrc's own API (`GET /packages/v2/labsamples`, `GET /labtests/v2/results`, `GET /labtests/v2/labtestdocument/{id}`).
4. The Metrc API needs an integrator key from Metrc Connect (the owner is in that gate: the TPI webinar, the test, the certificate) and the licensee's user key; v2 only; sandbox at sandbox-api-mi.metrc.com; pageSize 20; under 50,000 calls a month on the Standard plan; POST /packages/v2/testing is the first write, not idempotent, dedupe on Tag.

## The law to build, in three parts

### Part 1: the desk (schedule, sample, manifest, results)

1. THE LABS: a table `testing_labs` (id, name, portal_kind `tagleaf|slingr|other`, portal_url, med_license, au_license, turnaround_days, pickup_cutoff, active, stamps), seeded from his workbook's Driver Info tab and the research: SC Labs Michigan (med SC-000035, adult-use AU-SC-000129, portal `tagleaf`, https://lims.tagleaf.com/login?affiliate=can-lab-llc), ACT Laboratories (adult-use AU-SC-000152, med SC-000048, portal `slingr`, https://actcustomerportal.slingrs.io/prod/runtime/login.html), Lab Link (med SC-000037, adult-use AU-SC-000133), Viridis (med SC-000009, adult-use AU-SC-000113, active 0: closed 2025); editable in Admin, Lookups. Driver names, phones and license numbers from the workbook are personnel data: never seed them; the desk types a driver per manifest into fields that stay in the database.
2. THE ORDER: a table `test_orders` (id, lot_id (wet_weight_entries), source_package_tag (the lot package's METRC tag when known), item_name, license_value, market (`AU|MED`), lab_id, panel (`full_compliance|potency|rnd`), required_lab_test_batch (default `Raw Plant Material`), batch_grams (the lot's dry grams at scheduling), status (`scheduled|sampled|in_transit|at_lab|results_in|passed|failed|retest_scheduled|remediated|cancelled`), pickup_date, notes, stamps), one open order per lot package at a time. THE SCHEDULE screen (a station under the Admin PIN, `/testing`): the lots READY TO SCHEDULE (his pick: lot packages out of the dryer and trimmed or cryo'd, with no open order), each with its HBN, strain, farm, item, license and market, dry grams and the sample size the rule asks (0.5% of the batch, 14 g floor, rounded up to the lab's increment schedule when known; a 50 lb batch refuses in words with the rule number), a lab picker, a panel picker, a pickup date; Schedule creates the order and logs it. The tech still books the pickup in the lab's portal (TagLeaf's calendar or ACT's); the screen says so and links the lab's portal.
3. THE SAMPLE: a table `test_samples` (id, order_id, sample_tag (the METRC test-sample tag the sampler is handed), net_grams, sampled_at, sampler_name (the lab's sampler), grower_witness (a person on our side), chain_of_custody_ref, sealed_container_ref, attempt (1 for the first compliance sample, 2 and up for retests), stamps). THE SAMPLE screen: pick a scheduled order, type the tag, grams, sampler, the witness and the chain-of-custody reference, Save moves the order to `sampled` and logs it. The tag must be tag-shaped (THE TAG LAW, canon 0.30.38) and unused.
4. THE MANIFEST (his words: "even if it's a CSV result on the admin screens and not an open manifest to start"): from one or more sampled orders bound for the same lab on the same day, the desk produces two downloads and records them. (a) The METRC New Packages CSV for the sample packages in the shape of his workbook's Testing Autofill CSV, header row included per MI_IB_0103: `Tag Number, Location Name, Item Name, Use Same Item, Quantity, Unit Of Measure, Note, Actual Date, Expiration Date, Sell By Date, Use By Date, Is Donation, Template Ingredients Quantity, Template Ingredients Unit Of Measure, Package Number, Ingredient Quantity, Ingredient Unit Of Measure, Ingredient Finish Package, Ingredient Finish Date`, one row per sample: the sample tag, the location `In Testing`, Use Same Item true, the net grams as Quantity in `Grams`, the actual date, the source package tag as Package Number and the same grams as Ingredient Quantity. (b) A manifest worksheet (CSV and a printable page) for the Metrc transfer UI: transfer type `Testing Transfer (to SCF)`, the lab's license for the market, the sample tags and gross weights, the driver and vehicle fields the tech types, the route note. A `test_manifests` table (id, lab_id, license_value, manifest_date, metrc_manifest_number (typed after the UI step), driver fields, stamps) ties the samples; Mark sent moves the orders to `in_transit`, the manifest number typed later moves them to `at_lab`. The CSV lands at the export home (Admin, Pending Data Uploads) like the other METRC CSVs, with the mark-uploaded flow.
5. THE RESULTS: a table `test_results` (id, sample_id, test_type (the Michigan test types: `potency`, `foreign_matter`, `microbials`, `heavy_metals`, `chemical_residue`, `water_activity`, `mycotoxins`, `terpenes`, `other`), passed INTEGER, value NUMERIC, unit, qualifier, notes, result_date, stamps) and on the sample: `overall_passed`, `thc_total_pct`, `thca_pct`, `cbd_total_pct`, `water_activity_aw`, `coa_url` (the lab's COA page, lims.tagleaf.com/coa_/<id> or ACT's), `coa_file_id` (a METRC LabTestResultDocumentFileId when the mirror lands), `lab_sample_id` (the lab's own id, like ICM-250702-050), `results_at`. THE RESULTS screen: pick an order at the lab, type each test type's pass or fail and value, the potency figures, the COA link, the lab sample id; Save marks the sample and the order (`passed` or `failed`) and logs it. A failed order offers SCHEDULE RETEST (a new order attempt 2 on the same lot package, the rule quoted: two separate new samples must pass consecutively) and MARK REMEDIATED (a reason, the CRA approval reference, the date; the order to `remediated`, the next attempt required). The failure mode of a failed sample is the set of failed test types (the dashboard's "by failure mode" is by test type; a free-text reason rides beside it).
6. THE TAGS AND THE FUTURE API: every record keeps the METRC tags (source package, sample) as the join keys, and the tables carry the columns the Metrc mirror will fill (`metrc_lab_testing_state`, `metrc_state_date`, `metrc_result_id`, `coa_file_id`), NULL until the API lands. Nothing in Part 1 calls Metrc.

### Part 2: the dashboard (the console's Testing section)

The Testing section of the Harvest Console (a stub today) reads: NUMBER OF LOTS READY (ready to schedule, law 2), NUMBER OF LOTS SCHEDULED (orders in `scheduled`, `sampled`, `in_transit`, `at_lab`), FIRST TIME PASS RATE (attempt 1 samples with results: passed over all, as a percentage with the counts under it) BY LAB and within each lab BY FAILURE MODE (the failed test types of the first attempts), and BY STRAIN and within each strain BY FARM (the lot's strain and the HBN's farm), NUMBER OF LOTS WAITING FOR RETEST (orders `failed` with no later attempt, and `remediated` awaiting one), LINKS TO COAS BY PARENT TAG (a list, newest first: the source package tag, the lot, the lab, the date, the COA link), THC BY PARENT TAG (the same list with `thc_total_pct`, sortable). The season is the harvest year; the console day control does not apply (the section is the season's).

### Part 3: the Metrc mirror, read-only (built now, dark until the keys land)

1. A `src/metrcapi.ts` client: Basic auth from `METRC_INTEGRATOR_KEY` and `METRC_USER_KEY` (wrangler secrets, never vars, never in a file), base URL from a var `METRC_API_BASE` (sandbox by default in the default env, production under env.grip only when the owner sets it), pageSize 20, Retry-After honored, every call counted in a `metrc_api_calls` table (started_at, path, status, ms) so the monthly budget is visible in Admin.
2. A cron (the sync brief's pattern) that, when the keys exist, sweeps `GET /packages/v2/labsamples` and `GET /packages/v2/active` with lastModified windows (the last hour plus five minutes), then `GET /labtests/v2/results?packageId=` for changed samples, and writes the mirror columns of Part 1 (state, state date, result id, COA file id, THC and pass flags where the desk left them blank), logging every change; `GET /labtests/v2/labtestdocument/{id}` fetched lazily into R2 or a blob column when the owner rules where COAs live (a fork for later). No write to Metrc in this slice: `POST /packages/v2/testing` is designed (the payload from the sample record: Tag, Item from the source, UseSameItem true, Quantity, "Grams", ActualDate, Ingredients with the source tag and grams, RequiredLabTestBatches from `GET /labtests/v2/batches`), documented in the report, and left behind a switch that is off.
3. Admin gains a `[Metrc API]` page: the key state (present or absent, never the values), the last sweep, the call count this month, a `Test connection` button that calls `GET /facilities/v2` and reports in words.
