## The law to build

1. THE THIRD STAGE. Production Scheduling gains `prepack` beside `trim` and `cryo` (`SCHEDULE_STAGES.prepack`, title `Prepack`, a third tile on the landing, the same three panes: Available, Scheduled, Complete). THE FEED IS TEST PASSED WPR: the Available pane lists every package that is a cryo output (`wip_packages.source = 'cryo'`), of the WPR product category (its `package_type_code` maps to `WPR` through `cryo_type_categories`, SMALLS by default), released from the Testing Queue with the outcome `passed` (`status = 'released'`, `test_outcome = 'passed'`), with a live ledger item carrying a METRC tag and grams above zero, and no live prepack schedule row. Grouped by HBN like the other stages; each item reads the LAST SIX DIGITS OF ITS METRC TAG (the tag law: the tag when the ledger holds one, the code otherwise), the strain and the grams. Schedule prepack writes a `production_schedules` row: `stage = 'prepack'`, `wip_package_id` the package, `harvest_batch_id` its HBN, `status = 'scheduled'`, the effective date as the other stages take it. Scheduled and Complete read as the other surfaces do (the middle pane the scheduled jobs, the right pane the completed ones, each by the last six digits).
2. THE STATION. Production Operations gains `[Prepack]` (`/station/prepack`, the `production_ops` group, after Cryo Weight Entry; its own pipeline registry entry, canon L-4 data). Left pane: the scheduled prepack jobs, by the last six digits of the parent tag with the strain and the parent's grams remaining; pick one. Middle pane, the line form for the picked job: Target Weight/Unit, a select of the units in app_config `prepack_units` (`3.5,7,14,28` seeded, grams, editable in Admin, Lookups, app_config), THE DEFAULT IS THE LAST USED (the unit of the newest non-voided prepack line on the station, any job, any operator; 3.5 when none); Bags Produced, a whole number above zero; Tag Number, the METRC tag of the package these bags become (tag-shaped, 24 characters starting 1A, not yet on the ledger). Add line writes a `prepack_lines` row (job, parent package, unit_grams, bags, grams = bags times the unit rounded half up to whole grams, tag, carried_grams, carry_id, the timestamps) and the ledger: the parent's item loses the line's grams less any carried grams (`setItemQtyLocationStmts` with the reason `prepack line`), a new FG ledger item is born for the tag (item_kind FG, metrc_tag the typed tag, qty the line's grams in g, location app_config `prepack_location`, seeded `Prepack Room`), each with its change-log rows. A line is voided with a reason from its row (the grams return to the parent, the FG item is voided). The right pane, for the picked job: LBS PRODUCED BY BAG TYPE, one row per unit (bags, lbs at 448 two decimals, grams), TOTAL LBS PRODUCED, and the parent's grams remaining (live ledger qty) so the operator sees when Finish Pack is due.
3. FINISH PACK. A button on the picked job, refused in words while the job has no line. It computes the remainder R, the parent's live ledger grams. When R is above zero the page asks first: `R g remain on ...ABCDEF. Push it onto the next pack job?` with Yes and No. Yes writes a `prepack_carries` row (from_job, from_package, grams R, remaining R, status `open`). Either answer then runs THE FINISH LOOP ON THE PARENT: the parent's ledger item goes to zero with the reason (`prepack finish, R g carried` or `prepack finish, R g left`, or `prepack finish` when R was zero), the parent `wip_packages` row takes the terminal status `packed` (a new `WIP_STATUS`, out of every queue, kept as the record, beside `pasteurized` and `gone`), the schedule row completes (`completeScheduleStmts`), and two files pend in Pending Data Uploads: the Finish file (`package_finish`, Label the parent tag, ActualDate the farm day; the kind exists in config and the uploads page, its download lane returns for prepack parents) and the New Packages file of law 5. Either way the Finish file is produced, as he said.
4. THE CARRY IS THE LEDGER'S. While any carry is `open`, the line form of ANY job shows the prompt above its fields: `R g carried from ...ABCDEF (finished <date>). Use it first?` with Use it and Not now (Not now leaves the carry open; the prompt returns on the next line). Use it makes the next saved line consume the carry first: carried_grams = the smaller of the carry's remaining and the line's grams; the parent of this job is debited only the rest; the carry's remaining shrinks and its status turns `used` at zero; a line records its carry_id. The carry is the app's accounting only (his pick): the New Packages row of that line names this job's parent alone as the ingredient at the full bags times the unit, and the console's lbs count the line's full grams.
5. THE METRC FILES. At Finish Pack one New Packages file for the job in the owner's T3 autofill template (the cryo run's writer in `src/cryo.ts`, reused): one row per non-voided line, Tag Number the line's tag, Location Name app_config `prepack_location`, Item Name from app_config `prepack_items` (a `unit=Item name` list, seeded `3.5=PREPACK 3.5;7=PREPACK 7;14=PREPACK 14;28=PREPACK 28` for him to correct in Admin; a unit without an item refuses Finish Pack in words), Use Same Item false, Quantity the bags, Unit Of Measure Each, Actual Date the line's farm day, the ingredient the job's parent tag at bags times the unit in Grams. The Finish file as law 3. Both pend under the job's HBN with the kinds `new_packages` and `package_finish`.
6. THE CONSOLE. The Harvest Console gains a Prepack section (an open `hc-section`, after Testing, the last section on the page; `data-hc-section="prepack"`), the day the console's `?day=` control reads like Trimming's yesterday: bags produced by size (one figure per unit in `prepack_units`), Lbs produced (the day's non-voided lines' grams at 448), Jobs finished (the day's completed prepack schedules), DLH Yesterday, ONE BOX A DAY (his pick): a numbox posting to `/harvest/prepack-hours`, a `prepack_labor_hours` row per work date (no shift), behind the console's PIN like the other hours; Lbs/DLH = the lbs over the typed hours, blank until hours are typed (`type the hours` in words, `escriba las horas` in Spanish). The section's footnote says where the figures come from. The sheet does not replace this box in this slice (a later brief may, if the Labor tab carries prepack hours).
7. WHOLE GRAMS, 448. Every gram figure is whole (a line's grams round half up), every pound reads two decimals at 448; the units list may carry a decimal (3.5) and is shown as typed.
8. SPANISH SPEAKS on every new string (EN and ES).
