## The laws

1. MIGRATION `0087_the_hbn_entry.sql` (Postgres-portable; nothing before 0087 edited):
   - `ALTER TABLE harvest_batches ADD COLUMN material_form TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'bucked'`: the HBN's form, `spear` or `bucked`. Existing HBNs read bucked (the floor's 54 of 63 lots are bucked; the field node's spear default is the complaint); the owner's repair file sets any that should read spear.
   - `ALTER TABLE wet_weight_entries ADD COLUMN finished_at TEXT`: the instant Finish lot moved the lot off weighing. Backfill: for every live lot whose status is not weighing, the newest `record_change_log` row for that lot whose field is status and whose new value is not weighing (`changed_at`), else the lot's `updated_at`.
   - `lookup_values` `silo_number`: `INSERT OR IGNORE` the rows '6' (label "6 Arlington Dry Room", sort 6) and '7' (label "7 Blast Freezer(s)", sort 7), ids in the `a2000001-0001-4000-8000-00000000000N` series; the NA row (the ...006 row) gets `deleted_at` set (retired from the pick list, kept in data; existing HBNs keep their NA).
2. THE SEQUENCE. `src/config.ts` exports `SILO_SEQUENCE_SIZE = 7`. `src/db.ts` gets `listSiloSequence(db)`: the seven kind-silo spaces in the drying strip's order (`listSilos`), numbered 1 to 7 by position, so today 6 is the Arlington Dry Room and 7 is Blast Freezer(s), and a renamed space keeps its number by its place. `siloSpaceOfNumber(seq, n)` resolves a number to a space (id, name, kind, farm). Every place that matched `'Silo ' || planned_dryspc` by name resolves through the sequence instead (the four joins in `src/db.ts`, `plannedSpaceIdOf`, the Load Silo preselect, the silo tiles): resolve the numbers to ids in JS from the sequence and join on `dry_spaces.id`, or carry a CASE that maps '6' and '7' to those two names; either way one helper, not four copies.
3. THE DEFAULT SILO (his pick, "Next silo with room"). `nextSiloNumber(db)`: walk the sequence 1 to 7 and take the lowest-numbered space that is not filling, where filling means any live lot sits in it (`wet_weight_entries.actual_dryspc` = the space, status not weighing and not trimmed and not frozen-out: the statuses the drying strip counts as loaded or unloaded-in-place; the build reads `listSilos`/`siloLoads` for the exact set) or any open HBN is planned for it (`harvest_batches.status = 'open' AND planned_dryspc = N`). When all seven are busy, round robin: the number after the most recently created HBN's planned silo (numeric 1-7, `ORDER BY created_at DESC LIMIT 1`), 7 wrapping to 1; with no such HBN, 1. The number is advice on the page: no lock, no reservation; two people on the form at once may both see 3 and one of them picks again.
4. ACQUIRE HBN. The field becomes `{ name: "planned_dryspc", label: "Silo", input: "lookup", lookup: "silo_number", required }` preselected to `nextSiloNumber` on GET (the lookup shows 1-5, 6 Arlington Dry Room, 7 Blast Freezer(s); NA is gone from the list); its help: "Where this HBN's lots land and what the drying schedule plans for. The next silo with room is picked for you; change it before you Save." A new radio `{ name: "material_form", label: "Spear or Bucked", input: "radio", options Spear and Bucked, default "bucked" }` sits right above it; its help: "Bucked lots are weighed into the silo as WIP. Spear lots hang (Hang in Place at Penn North, the Arlington Dry Room at Arlington) as SPR and the silo is for the schedule." `createHarvestBatch` requires both ("Pick a silo." / "Pick Spear or Bucked."), writes `material_form`, and mints the HBN's sixth segment from the number as today (1 to 7; NA only when a METRC name or the sheet gives none). The Admin Manual HBN form gets the same two fields with the same default. The sheet sync and the METRC adopt leave `material_form` at the column default.
5. PENN NORTH HARVEST'S START LOT. "Move To" and the "Spear or Bucked" radio leave the header. In their place one read-only line under the HBN select, filled by the station's script from the picked HBN and rendered server-side for the picked one: "Bucked: lands in Silo 3 as WIP" or "Spear: hangs in Hang in Place as SPR", with a muted "change on the HBN" link to the HBN's Admin pencil (`/record/harvest_batches/:id/edit`). `createWetWeight` reads the HBN: `material_form` from the HBN; the space: bucked goes to `siloSpaceOfNumber(planned_dryspc)` (an HBN with no numeric silo refuses: "This HBN has no silo. Set it on the HBN first."), spear goes to the farm's holding space (`fieldHoldingSpaceId`, PNN Hang in Place). The truck law is unchanged (the Truck # field stays; a truck lot still finishes in transit and receives by its form); the repeat-tap guard keys on the HBN and the space as before. The posted `actual_dryspc` and `material_form`, if any client still sends them, are ignored.
6. THE FIELD NODE. `ensureWeighingLotStmts` reads the HBN too: `material_form` from the HBN; the space: bucked to `siloSpaceOfNumber(planned_dryspc)` (refuse the tote with "This HBN has no silo. Set it on the HBN first." when none), spear to `fieldHoldingSpaceId(destination)` as today. The send (Finish Load) mints by the lot's form and space as the Penn North path does (the silo branch for bucked, WIP into the silo under PNN's grow license; the hang branch for spear). The HBN select on the field screen shows each HBN's form and silo in its option text ("26274-PNS-... Bucked Silo 1").
7. THE HBN QUEUE (the floor lead's note). Finish lot writes `finished_at` (the lot's status leaves weighing: Finish lot, the field send, the truck receive, any path that sets a non-weighing status). `NO_FINISHED_LOT_SQL` keeps an HBN while it has a weighing lot, or no live lot, or a lot whose `finished_at` falls on or after the start of the farm's today (06:00 Eastern of the current farm day, `farmDayBounds(today).startIso`), so an HBN finished this morning stays on the list through the farm day whatever day its lot started. A weighing lot whose `created_at` is before the start of yesterday's farm day shows an age flag on its queue row: ", weighing since 2026-09-28" (EN; ES "pesando desde"). The pick, the tote HBN select and the Demote path are unchanged.
8. THE PENCILS. The HBN edit form: "Silo" (`planned_dryspc`, lookup, `optional: false`, the current value preselected and a NULL shown as a blank option so a save never writes "1" by accident) and "Spear or Bucked" (`material_form`, a radio, the same two words). The lot edit form: "Move To" is renamed "Dry space" (the Admin correction tool keeps its sync of the ledger item to the space; it is off the floor); "Spear or Bucked" becomes the same radio. No HBN string is re-minted by an edit.
9. WORDS. EN and ES for everything above in `src/i18n.ts` ("Silo", "Spear or Bucked" exist; new: the two helps, "lands in {space} as WIP", "hangs in {space} as SPR", "change on the HBN", "This HBN has no silo. Set it on the HBN first.", "weighing since", "Dry space", the lookup labels). No em or en dash anywhere.

## Assumptions for his word

- The HBN's form defaults to Bucked on Acquire HBN and on existing HBNs (the floor is bucked 54 to 9; his complaint is the spear default). Say Spear if the default should stay Spear.
- "Remove move-to altogether" is read as the floor: Start lot and the field node lose every choice of space and form; the Admin lot pencil keeps a renamed "Dry space" field as the correction tool. Say so if the pencil's field should go too.
- "Filling" for the default silo is read as any live lot in the space or any open HBN planned for it; capacity in pounds plays no part (a silo with one small lot is busy). Say so if capacity should count.
- An HBN with no numeric silo (the thirteen NULL and four NA on the floor, and future sheet or METRC mints) refuses a bucked lot until its silo is set on the HBN; the Scribe hands a repair file for the open ones (26082 to 1, 26081 to 5, the rest by his list) after the merge.
- The sixth segment of a new HBN reads 6 or 7 for the Arlington Dry Room and the Blast Freezer(s); the data feed (`src/feeds.ts`) reads those numbers as silos, not "hang".
- The HBN queue's age flag is words on the row, not a filter: nothing is hidden for being old.
