## The law

1. THE COLUMN. Migration 0099 adds `harvest_batches.customer TEXT` (NULL for Grip's own lots). A lot with a customer is a TOLL LOT: another company's material that Grip's floor works. The migration touches no row.
2. WHERE IT IS SET. The manual HBN form (Admin Settings, Manual HBNs) gains "Customer (toll lot)", a text field, blank by default, beside "Test HBN"; a lot cannot be both (refused in words). The Edit form of a batch shows the field too. No other door sets it.
3. THE LINE. A toll lot holds a real place in the line like any lot of Grip's own: the auto sequencer counts it (0095's 1+MAX over `is_test = 0` already does, since a toll lot is not a test). Its HBN text follows H1 (ends in -YY###, does not already exist).
4. THE FIGURES. Everywhere a test is left out of Grip's own figures, a toll lot is left out too: the season and the console's totals (`console.ts`), dry per plant and Assign Plant Counts (`plantcounts.ts`), Dry Weight by Strain (`drystrain.ts`), Floor vs METRC's Grip side (`floormetrc.ts`), the harvest weight reconciliation (`harvestreconciliation.ts`, shows a toll lot in a section of its own), and any other query that filters `is_test = 0` for a yield or a season figure; the rule is written once as a shared SQL fragment (`OWN_LOT_SQL` or the like) and every such query uses it. Where the floor WORKS a lot (the HBN queue, wet weight, unload, trim, the cryo queue, the dashboard's queues, the trim bars), a toll lot shows like any other, with its customer named beside the HBN ("GreenCo") in the same place the test mark sits today.
5. THE REPORTS. Dry Weight by Strain and the season report gain a line "Toll lots: {customer} {n} lots, {g} g dry" under their own Total when toll lots fall in the range, so the figure is visible without being in Grip's totals; the CSV carries it as its own row. Floor vs METRC lists toll lots in a section of their own.
6. NOTHING ELSE MOVES. Tests keep their law. No lot changes in the migration; the two GreenCo lots are converted by a repair after the merge (customer GreenCo, `is_test` 0, serials into the line, the texts above, 227,000 g in, the packages sorted between the lots by the owner's list).
