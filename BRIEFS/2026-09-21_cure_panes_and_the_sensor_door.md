## The law to build

1. THE TWO PANES: on every Drying Operations page that carries the silo strip (the pages `siloStrip` renders on) and on the Harvest Console beside the FFZ tile, two panes titled `CSA` and `CSB` (his names, no expansion). Each lists its lots as rows: LOT (the HBN's short id and the lot's serial, linked to the lot), WEIGHT in pounds (the lot's dry weight, `wet_weight_entries.dry_weight_grams`, at 448 g a pound, two decimals; the ledger package's live grams in a smaller second figure), TIME IN (now less the pane's in-time, as `1d 03:24` style, the running-clock shape the silos use, ticking client-side like the silo clocks), MOISTURE AT IN-TIME (the latest internal reading at or before the in-time), LATEST MOISTURE with its timestamp (the newest internal reading for the lot), and a CONDITIONS line for the room the lot sits in (law 4).
2. CSA holds the lots that have left the dryer and are not yet scheduled for trim: `wet_weight_entries` in status `unloaded` (THE UNLOADED QUEUE, canon 0.30.9x; frozen lots are excluded) with no live `production_schedules` row of stage `trim` (any status); the in-time is the unload's finish (`unload_sessions.finished_at` of the lot's latest finished session; failing that, the change-log row that set the status to unloaded). A lot leaves CSA the moment a trim schedule row is created for it ("Empty on lot scheduled for trim").
3. CSB holds the lots scheduled for trim and not yet scheduled for cryo: `wet_weight_entries` (status `unloaded` or `trimmed`) with a live `production_schedules` row of stage `trim`, and none of their trim containers taken into a cryo run (`cryo_run_inputs` joined to a live scheduled or complete cryo `production_schedules` row, canon 0.30.110); the in-time is that trim schedule row's `scheduled_at`. A lot leaves CSB the moment any of its containers is taken into a cryo run ("Empty on lot scheduled for cryo"). A lot voided or frozen leaves both.
4. THE MOISTURE READINGS come from the compliance console's internal testing (`internal_test_results`, canon 0.30.22): rows matched to the lot by `hbn` (the lot's HBN) or by `package_tag` equal to the lot's harvest package tag; the analyte is `moisture` where present, else `water_activity` (label which it is; water activity shows as `0.62 aw`, moisture as `11.4 %`). "At in-time" is the newest reading with `tested_at` at or before the in-time; "latest" is the newest reading overall; "no reading" in words when none.
5. THE SENSOR DOOR (his pick): a new table `sensor_readings` (id, room TEXT, temperature_f NUMERIC, humidity_pct NUMERIC, source TEXT, read_at TEXT, received_at TEXT, raw TEXT) and a push route `POST /sensors/readings` that accepts JSON `{ "room": "CSA", "temperature_f": 68.2, "humidity_pct": 58, "read_at": "2026-09-21T15:00:00Z", "source": "silo-ops" }` or an array of up to 100 such objects, validates (room in a known list, numbers in range, read_at parseable), stores each, answers `{ ok: true, stored: n }`, and refuses in words otherwise. The route is a machine door under THE SERVICE ACTOR (canon 0.30.140): it answers a request that carries no login email only when the Access application's Service Auth policy admitted it (a second service token, `sensor-bridge`, issued by the owner in Cloudflare Access beside `silo-ops-cron` and `trimatron-puller`), and the service actor's door list in `src/service.ts` gains `POST /sensors/readings` alone; a logged-in person may post it too (a test bridge). The rooms: `CSA` and `CSB` to start, values in data (app_config `cure_rooms`, a comma list), so a sensor can be named for a room without a deploy. The panes' CONDITIONS line reads the newest reading for the pane's room: `68 F · 58 % · 4 min ago`, red words when the reading is older than an hour, `no sensor yet` before the first. Nothing pulls from Corey's app in this slice; his side posts to the door.
6. THE FACE ON THE CONSOLE: the two panes sit in the Drying section beside the FFZ tile, the same card style; on the drying pages they stand under the silo strip. Spanish speaks on every label.
