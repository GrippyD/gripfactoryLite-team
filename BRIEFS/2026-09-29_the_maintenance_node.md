## The law to build

1. THE BUTTON AND THE LANDING. `bar-actions` gains `<a class="maintenance-link" href="/maintenance">Maintenance</a>` before Reports, every page, every view, no PIN. `/maintenance` is the landing: the title, the drying pane exactly as the console draws it (`siloStrip` with the cure tiles, the flags, the clock and live scripts) carrying the new check lines of law 3, and three buttons under it: Load Silo (to `/station/load_silo`), Lab Sample (to `/maintenance/lab_sample`), Trouble Tickets (to `/maintenance/trouble_tickets`, a stub page in the console's stub words). The Home page's operations map does not change.
2. THE INTERVALS. Two lists in Admin, Lookups, under Harvest Operations, one page `/admin/lookups/silo_check_intervals` titled "Internal Silo Testing Interval", with the aW list and the MC list side by side: each list is `lookup_values` rows of category `silo_check_interval_aw` or `silo_check_interval_mc`, the `value` a whole number of hours, the `label` "Check n", the `sort_order` the position; the top line of each list is the add form, "Add a Time" with one field, [Hours from last check], and a + button; the first row reads "from the start of the silo", every later row "from the last check"; a row can be removed (soft delete with the reason "removed", the rest renumbered on read, not rewritten). An empty list means no check of that kind and no line on the tiles.
3. THE SCHEDULE AND THE LINES. For an open load and a check kind, the requests made so far are the `lab_sample_requests` rows of that load and kind with `requested_at` set, ordered by it; with `n` of them and the hours list `h[0..k]`, the next check's number is `n + 1` and its due instant is `loaded_at + h[0]` when `n` is 0, else the last `requested_at` plus `h[min(n, k)]` (past the end of the list the last interval repeats). A pure function `nextCheckDue` in `src/silo.ts` (or a new `src/labsample.ts`) computes it and is tested. On every tile drawn from `silo_loads` (the silos, the Arlington Dry Room, the freezers; the cure panes wait for V7) each load's block gains two lines under its clock: "Next aW check in" and "Next MC check in", each a countdown (`<span data-countdown data-due="<ms>">0d 04:12:33</span>` ticked once a second by the clock script's sibling) when the kind has a list; the tile's line is the earliest due among the space's open loads per kind (his "shortest remaining interval for that silo") and names the lot when the space holds several. When the due instant is at or past now, the line becomes THE SIREN: the fifteenth line-art icon, `siren` in `src/consoleicons.ts` (a beacon on a base with three rays, the lamp carrying `data-icon-siren-lamp`, drawn to V2's contract and added to the proofs sheet and its test), in the waste red and throbbing on the waste keyframes, with the words "aW check due" or "MC check due" and the check number; it stays until a request for that lot and kind is made on Lab Sample (the request moves the due instant forward, so the countdown returns). The countdown script swaps a line to the siren the moment it reaches zero without a reload; `/silos/live.json` carries per space `checks: [{ kind, lot, checkNumber, dueAt }]` and the live paint refreshes the lines every 30 s, so a request made on Lab Sample clears the siren on every open tile within the refresh. A paused clock does not pause the schedule (the assumption below).
4. LAB SAMPLE, `/maintenance/lab_sample`, three panes. LEFT, "Drying": every open load, one row per load and kind that has a list, reading "Silo 3 / Lot 26070", the strain, the kind, the next check number and its countdown or its siren, with a `>>` that schedules it; a row leaves the left pane while its check is scheduled or requested. MIDDLE, "Schedule Silo/Lot Check #": the scheduled and requested rows, each reading the space, the lot, the kind and the check number; a scheduled row carries the button [Sample requested], which stamps `requested_at` and `requested_by`, sets the status `requested`, and clears the siren everywhere (the schedule now counts from this stamp), and `<<`, which unschedules (soft delete, reason "unscheduled"); a requested row reads "requested at <time>" and carries `>>`, the lab's hand, with an optional result (aW as a number from 0 to 1, MC as a percent), which fulfills it. A request also fulfills itself when an internal result for the lot's HBN and the kind's analyte (`water activity`, or the new `moisture content`) lands with `tested_at` after `requested_at`: the loader marks it fulfilled with that result's id, so the instrument import is enough. RIGHT, "Completed": fulfilled rows newest first with the value and the time, and `<<` to return one to requested (a mistake). Every move is logged in `record_change_log` under `lab_sample_requests`, field `status`.
5. THE TABLE, the migration: `lab_sample_requests (id, silo_load_id NOT NULL, dry_space_id NOT NULL, wet_weight_entry_id NOT NULL, kind TEXT NOT NULL ('aw' or 'mc'), check_number INTEGER NOT NULL, status TEXT NOT NULL ('scheduled', 'requested', 'fulfilled'), scheduled_at, scheduled_by, requested_at, requested_by, fulfilled_at, fulfilled_by, result_value NUMERIC, result_unit TEXT, internal_test_result_id TEXT, notes TEXT, created_at, created_by, updated_at, updated_by, deleted_at, deleted_by, delete_reason)` with an index on `(silo_load_id, kind, requested_at)` and a partial unique index on `(silo_load_id, kind, check_number) WHERE deleted_at IS NULL`. A lab hand's result writes an `internal_test_results` row (`source` `lab_sample`, the analyte, the value, the unit, the HBN, `tested_at` now) and points at it. `moisture content` joins the internal analyte aliases (`mc`, `moisture`, `moisture content`, `mc%`) so an instrument file can carry it.
