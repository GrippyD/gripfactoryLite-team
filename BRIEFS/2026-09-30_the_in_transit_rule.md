## The ruling (owner, 2026-09-30, verbatim)

"When a silo is scheduled, it should leave the in-transit list. When it's moved to in transit, it should expose every HBN that was on the load to the silo scheduler as individual lines. If a load that has an HBN on it and that same HBN was already listed in the silo scheduler, that weight accumulates together (essentially the records need to continue to merge under the HBN/Lot tag) until the scheduler schedules it to a silo."

## The laws

1. MIGRATION `0083_field_in_transit.sql`: `ALTER TABLE harvest_batches ADD COLUMN field_closed_at TEXT`; `ALTER TABLE harvest_batches ADD COLUMN field_closed_by TEXT`; `ALTER TABLE field_loads ADD COLUMN received_at TEXT`; `ALTER TABLE field_loads ADD COLUMN received_by TEXT`. Null on every existing row. Postgres-portable SQL; nothing before 0083 edited.
2. THE EXPOSURE AT SEND. A weighing lot of an ARL or PNS HBN that carries at least one live tote on a load in transit is a Dry Queue line. A new `listDryQueueLots` in `src/db.ts` returns the finished lots of `listBarnWetLots` plus those weighing lots (the same columns; `truck_number` null on a weighing lot; `finished_at` carrying the earliest `sent_at` of the loads its totes ride, the wait's origin); Internal Logistics and `listLotParts` read it. `listBarnWetLots` and Load Silo do not change: a weighing lot is never loadable. The line groups as today (`logisticsLotView`, one row per HBN with its parts); a row whose parts include a weighing lot carries `data-logistics-in-transit` and reads, where the trucks read, "in transit, {n} loads" (ES "en transito, {n} cargas"; "1 load" / "1 carga"), n the count of in-transit loads carrying that lot's totes; its grams are the lot's total as today.
3. THE MERGE. A later load carrying the same HBN, sent while its weighing lot stands, adds its totes to that lot (as "+" already does) and the line grows; the loads count rises. No new lot is minted while the HBN's weighing lot stands, so the records merge under the HBN.
4. THE PASS FINISHES THE LOT. `>>` Pass lot on an HBN whose parts include a weighing lot finishes that lot first, in the pass's own batch, exactly as YES finishes one (`singleLotFinishStmts` with the plant count skipped: weighing to drying, `harvest_file_seq`, the born package minted; reason `finish lot`), then writes the pass row as today. The HBN then reads Scheduled with its parts, `>` opens Load Silo picked, `<<` Return lot voids the pass and the finished lot stays on the Dry Queue as today. The HBN stays open to field weight: the next "+" for it mints a new weighing lot (`ensureWeighingLotStmts` as it stands), a new part, its own line at its send.
5. THE LOAD LEAVES THE IN-TRANSIT LIST. After the pass row is written (`scheduleLogisticsLot`, the same batch or right after it), every in-transit load carrying a live tote of that HBN is checked, and a load is received when none of its live totes sits on a weighing lot and every HBN on it has a live pass (stage `internal_logistics`, scheduled or complete, not deleted) with `scheduled_at` at or after the load's `sent_at`: `status` `received`, `received_at` now, `received_by` the actor, logged (reason `field load received`). `listRecentSentFieldLoads` becomes `listInTransitFieldLoads`: status `in_transit` alone, newest first, however old (the two-day window of V15's assumption goes); a received load is in the record and its log and has no `<<`. `<<` on an in-transit load reopens it as today; a pass returned later does not un-receive a load.
6. YES CLOSES THE HBN. Finish Load YES with Confirm, besides finishing the checked HBNs' lots, writes `field_closed_at` now and `field_closed_by` the actor on each checked batch (logged, reason `field hbn closed`). `FIELD_OPEN_HBN_SQL` becomes `AND b.field_closed_at IS NULL`: the field select lists an ARL or PNS open batch while it is not closed, whatever its lots; "+" and the tote change refuse a closed HBN as they refuse today ("That HBN is not on this list."). The Demote of a lot of that HBN on the Admin queues (drying back to weighing, the admin approval) clears `field_closed_at` and `field_closed_by` (logged, reason `demoted`), so the HBN returns to the select. A lot finished by the pass closes nothing.
7. THE WORDS, EN and ES, ASCII: "in transit" / "en transito", "{n} loads" / "{n} cargas", "1 load" / "1 carga"; the reasons `field load received`, `field hbn closed` in `SYSTEM_REASON`; the status `received` beside `open` and `in_transit` in `FIELD_LOAD_STATUS`.
8. NOTHING ELSE MOVES. Penn North's node and its auto pass, Load Silo, the drying surfaces, the unload, the files and the console untouched. `scripts/smoke.mjs` moves its pin to 83 and extends the field walk: after Finish Load NO on the first load (an ARL HBN and a PNS HBN, both weighing), the Dry Queue lists each as a line marked in transit while Load Silo's pane lists neither; a second load with the ARL HBN sent, the ARL line one line with both loads' grams and "2 loads"; `>>` on the ARL HBN: its lot drying with a born package, the row Scheduled, the second load received and off the pane, the first load still in transit (its PNS HBN open); `>>` on the PNS HBN: the first load received, the pane empty; a third load with the ARL HBN: "+" mints a new lot (the HBN still on the select), Finish Load YES on it: the HBN closed and off the select, "+" refused; the Demote of its lot on the Admin queues: the HBN back on the select.

## Assumptions for his word

- "The in-transit list" is the Loads sent pane. A load leaves it at the pass of the last HBN it carries; a load carrying one HBN closed by YES and one still open stays until both are passed.
- The pass finishes a weighing lot the way YES does: the plant count skipped (Load Silo asks), the born package minted as V15 mints it.
- A line's grams are the lot's total, so the totes of a load still open count on it once any load carrying the HBN is in transit; the loads count names the sent loads only.
- The wait on an in-transit line counts from the first send.
- The two-day window on the Loads sent pane goes; an in-transit load shows until received, however old, which is the truth of the floor.
- Load Silo never lists a weighing lot; on the field node the pass and YES are the only doors from weighing to drying.
- A pass returned with `<<` after a load was received leaves the load received; the lot stays finished on the Dry Queue as today.
- Penn North is untouched: its HBNs never carry `field_closed_at`.
