## The law to build

1. THE FOUR PANES, `[Inventory Gate]`. The Testing section's body becomes four panes left to right, Ready for Testing, Scheduled for Testing, In Testing, Passed Testing, each with its line-art icon (the `hc-icon` at about 120 px on a computer, 72 px on a phone) and the pounds centered under it (pounds big, grams small, the house's `weightPair`), and the package count small beneath ("12 packages"). Live, never the picked day: the `day` and `dayPicked` inputs drop out of `testingSection`. The gate set is the live packages (`gone_at IS NULL`) of the gate types under the farms' licenses, where the type is the item's first token before ` - ` upper-cased and the gate types are `app_config` `gate_type_codes`, default `BUD, WPR, PRE, MOB`; WBP, WIP, SHA, MIX, TRI, BIO and the rest never count. Grams: `uom` `g` is `quantity`; `uom` `ea` is `quantity` times the unit grams from `app_config` `gate_unit_grams`, default `PRE=28, MOB=70` (ruling 22); an `ea` item whose type has no unit grams counts its packages but adds no pounds, and the pane says "n packages without a unit weight" in a muted line. The four:
   - READY FOR TESTING: `lab_test_status = 'NotSubmitted'` and pasteurized (`isPasteurizedTag`: a live `wip_packages` row with `source = 'cryo'` and `UPPER(package_code)` equal to the tag), and no open desk order on the tag. A cryo output of a gate type not yet in the mirror counts as ready at its `cryo_weight_grams` (else `weight_grams`), since METRC has not seen it (the assumption below).
   - SCHEDULED FOR TESTING: the desk's orders in `TEST_CONSOLE_SCHEDULED`: an order with a `source_package_tag` in the mirror at the mirror's grams, else at its `batch_grams`; one package once.
   - IN TESTING: `lab_test_status = 'SubmittedForTesting'`.
   - PASSED TESTING: `lab_test_status` in (`TestPassed`, `RetestPassed`).
   A failed package (`TestFailed`, `RetestFailed`) is in none of the four; the doc's gate has no pane for it and the desk holds it.
2. THE QUALITY STATS, three panes under the four and as wide as the four together: First Time Fails, First Time Tests, FTQ Rate. The season's first-time tests are `external_test_results` rows with `attempt = 1`, `deleted_at IS NULL`, a `status` that reads passed or failed (the parser's `DeskOutcome` words), and the harvest year of the console: a row belongs to the year when its `hbn` starts with the year's last two digits, else when `entered_testing` falls in the year. First Time Tests is their count; First Time Fails the count with a failed status; FTQ Rate is passed over all as a whole percent with one decimal, and reads `NA` when there are no tests. Each stat carries a muted sub, "this season" and the count of tests behind the rate. Empty: the three read 0, 0 and NA with one muted line under them: "No first-time tests on the sheet this season." A test pins each number on a fixture of six rows.
3. THE FAILURE MODE ANALYSIS, one pane as wide as the three: a pie of the failed first-time tests by `failure_mode` (an empty mode reads "unnamed"), each segment's share by count, drawn as inline SVG paths in a 100 by 100 frame (no library; a single mode is a full ring; the colors eight fixed hues in `src/theme.ts` under `.hc-pie-1` to `.hc-pie-8`, cycling), with a legend at its side listing each mode, its count and its percent. A click on a segment or its legend line pulls the segment free (a `transform: translate` of 6 units along the segment's bisector, from `data-hc-pie-dx` and `data-hc-pie-dy` the server computes) and shows beside the pie the strains of that mode with their share of the mode's failures, the sheet's `strain` and the batch's market strain both when they differ (written "A / B"; the batch found by `hbn`); a second click puts it back; one segment free at a time. The list is server-rendered per segment and hidden until the click; the script toggles, nothing fetches. Empty: "No failed first-time tests on the sheet this season."

## Assumptions for his word

- The gate types default to BUD, WPR, PRE and MOB; the list is his to change in Admin without a slice.
- A cryo output the mirror has not pulled yet reads as Ready at its cryo weight (the pull is hourly at a quarter past).
- A package with an open desk order leaves Ready and sits in Scheduled until METRC says Submitted.
- Failed packages sit in no pane; the desk carries them.
- The season is the console's harvest year; the sheet's `hbn` prefix names it, the entered date the fallback.
- The pie's share is by count of failed first-time tests (the doc says "number of Test Samples"); grams ride as a sub.
- Until the sheet lands (his `MS_CLIENT_SECRET`, or a file in `DROPS/`), the stats and the pie show their empty words; the four panes read the mirror from the first deploy.
