## Why

Twice in the meetings of 10/7 and 10/12 the question "how many liters of distillate do we have for sure" went unanswered: about 480 on one count, a figure in the 500s on another, plus 94 more, against a physical count of 485 reported Thursday with "a few liters" still off. The only liters in the app today are Zoho sales lines (demand, not stock). The numbers live in a SharePoint workbook (Master Disti Tracking, one per harvest year) where the processors' grams are summed by hand. This brief gives the app liters on hand as a number with a date and a variance, not a sheet total. REVISED 2026-10-12 on the owner's word that Zoho is liter accurate and now names the harvest year in the item name ("2025 distillate"): Zoho, which the app already mounts, is the source of liters on hand, so the build reads it and does not re-key stock by hand.

## The law

0. THE ZOHO LEG (READ FIRST, it changes laws 1 to 3). Liters on hand come from the mounted Zoho inventory: `listings` rows with source zoho, a market UOM of L, and `market_qty` as the liters on hand (columns from `migrations/0039_zoho_inventory.sql`; read `src/zohoinv.ts`, whose mount already keeps every Active item with stock on hand, and its category and UOM rules), whose `item_name` contains the word distillate (any case) and a four digit harvest year (2025, 2026), the year read from the name. The report groups by harvest year then by item and shows the Zoho liters on hand per item and per year, the mount's date (the drop the figures came from), and the physical counts and the variance against them (law 1's counts table). Zoho decides liters on hand; the ledger below is reduced to the physical counts and, optionally, the processors' finished liters (the production leg): the `shipped` and `adjust` movement kinds are dropped (Zoho's sales and adjustments already move its stock), `finished` stays only as liters a processor made, for the production against receipts comparison. An item whose name has no year is listed under Year unknown and flagged. If a mounted item's UOM is not L the row is listed and flagged, never converted by guess. The remaining laws stand with this change: the table `distillate_movements` keeps only kind `finished`, the report adds the Zoho columns, the entry page adds counts and finished liters and no stock movements.
0b. OWNER RULING 2026-10-28 (canon 0.30.562), superseding law 0's line that keeps only kind `finished`: distillate_movements kinds are `finished`, `shipped` and `adjust`, all three ratified (resolves gate note A2 on PR #1). Law 1 stands as written. Zoho liters remain the on-hand figure per law 0.

1. TWO TABLES, migration `0124_distillate_ledger.sql`. `distillate_movements (id TEXT PRIMARY KEY, moved_on TEXT NOT NULL, harvest_year TEXT NOT NULL, processor TEXT NOT NULL REFERENCES processors(id), kind TEXT NOT NULL, grams NUMERIC NOT NULL, ref TEXT, note TEXT, entered_by TEXT, created_at TEXT NOT NULL, voided_at TEXT)`; `kind` is one of `finished` (distillate a processor made), `shipped` (sold or returned out), `adjust` (a signed correction, note required). `distillate_counts (id TEXT PRIMARY KEY, counted_on TEXT NOT NULL, harvest_year TEXT NOT NULL, processor TEXT REFERENCES processors(id), grams NUMERIC NOT NULL, counted_by TEXT, note TEXT, created_at TEXT NOT NULL, voided_at TEXT)`. Indexes on `(harvest_year, processor, moved_on)` and `(harvest_year, counted_on)`. Grams are the stored unit (the sheet's unit); liters are grams over 1,000, shown to two decimals. A movement is never edited or deleted: a mistake is voided (`voided_at`) and re-entered, and the void is written to `record_change_log` once for the record.
2. ON HAND IS COMPUTED. For a harvest year and processor: on hand = finished minus shipped plus adjust, over the unvoided movements. The Distillate on Hand report shows, per processor and harvest year: finished liters, shipped liters, on hand liters, the newest physical count (date and liters), and the variance (count minus ledger as of the count date), with the grand totals across processors and across harvest years. A variance bigger than one liter is flagged.
3. THE REPORT. `/reports/distillate_on_hand` on the standing report pattern (Reports menu, EN and ES), a harvest year picker (blank all), an "As of" date (default today; movements and counts after it are left out), CSV on the standing control. Read only. A second page section lists the last 20 movements and counts, newest first.
4. THE ENTRY PAGE. `/ops/distillate` (the Ops menu next to the toll lots, admin role only): add a movement or a count with a date, harvest year, processor (a picker read from the processors lookup of law 1a: active, not deleted rows, by name; a deleted or inactive processor stays shown on its old rows), kind, liters typed in liters (stored as grams, liters times 1,000, rounded to the gram), a reference and a note; void a row with a reason. Server-validated, refused when the liters are not a number, zero for a movement, or the date is in the future. No bulk import page in this brief (the opening balances ride a Scribe repair).
5. THE BOWHOUSE LINK. The yield column sits on the BowHouse row of the processors lookup. Where T1's toll lot records liters returned from a processor (at the 0.30.561 pin T1 records none, so this column reads blank as written below; no new source is invented), the entry page offers "from toll lot" as the reference and the report shows liters per pound of input beside the ledger for that processor: the yield the meetings had to guess at (about 70 lb of biomass per liter was quoted from the 26 H sheet). Read only from T1's tables; if T1 recorded no returned liters, the column reads blank. Do not write to T1's tables.
6. THE THURSDAY REPORT IS NOT THIS BRIEF. The weekly processor performance report asked for in the meetings reads from this ledger later; keep the ledger's reads reusable (one function per question, no HTML inside it).
7. NOTHING ELSE MOVES. Zoho, METRC, the toll lot pages, the feeds, the cron strings and the existing reports stay as they are. No cron job.

## Performance (a standing law, 0.30.438)

The ledger is small, but each read goes through the two indexes with bound values; no function on the big side of a join; at most 100 bound parameters per statement. The Scribe times the report and the entry page on the live-data bench; anything over one second is a defect.
