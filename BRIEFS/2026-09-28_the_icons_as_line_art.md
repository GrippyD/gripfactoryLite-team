## The law to build

1. THE MODULE, `src/consoleicons.ts`, new, importing nothing. It exports:
   - `type ConsoleIconName`, the union of the fourteen keys in the doc's order: `rad`, `cryo`, `bulk-bud`, `prepack`, `shake`, `waste`, `total`, `ready`, `scheduled`, `in-testing`, `passed`, `shipping`, `submitted`, `accepted`.
   - `CONSOLE_ICON_NAMES`, the fourteen as a readonly array in that order.
   - `CONSOLE_ICON_LABELS`, the English default label per key: Rad, Cryo, Bulk Bud, Prepack, Shake, Waste, Total, Ready for Testing, Scheduled for Testing, In Testing, Passed Testing, Shipping today, Submitted, Accepted. The panes translate their own labels later and pass them in; this module knows no locale.
   - `CONSOLE_ICON_STROKE`, the one stroke weight, `3`, used by every icon's root; a detail line may use `2`; nothing thinner. One constant, so his eye can move every icon at once.
   - `TRAILER_CARGO`, `{ x, y, width, height }` in the shipping icon's user units: the empty rectangle inside the trailer where V5 draws the stacked bar. Exported so V5 draws inside it without reading the path.
   - `consoleIconSvg(name, options?)` returning the inline SVG string; `options.label` overrides the aria-label, `options.className` appends classes. An empty label (`""`) makes the icon decorative: `aria-hidden="true"` in place of `role` and `aria-label`.
   The contract of every string `consoleIconSvg` returns:
   - the root `<svg class="hc-icon hc-icon-<name>" data-hc-icon="<name>" viewBox="0 0 100 100" role="img" aria-label="<label>" focusable="false" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="3" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round">`, no `width` or `height` attribute (the pane sizes it by CSS); the shipping icon alone may take `viewBox="0 0 160 100"` so the trailer has length.
   - line art in the ink: every color is `currentColor` or `none`; no hex, no `rgb(`, no named color, no `<style>`, no `<image>`, no `<foreignObject>`, no `<script>`, no `id`, no `<defs>`, no `<use>`, no gradient, no clip (a page draws the same icon more than once; ids would collide). Fills only where the picture needs a solid: text, a dot, the Cryo's bars; those are `fill="currentColor"`, with `fill-opacity` where a lighter tone is wanted.
   - the words, and only these, as `<text>` with `fill="currentColor"` and `stroke="none"`, `font-family="inherit"`, `font-weight="800"`: CRYO, AMAPS and UV in the Cryo; "Pretest checks" on the pad; "Today" on the calendar leaf; "COA" on the sheet; "PLUG" on the phone. No other icon carries text.
   - the hooks, each on the part the later slice will style: `data-icon-rad-body` on the Rad's outer cabinet path (the part that throbs green in V10); `data-icon-cryo-flow` on each of the three bars, with the values `meter-cryo`, `meter-amaps`, `cryo-uv` (the parts that turn burgundy and move in V10), the bars drawn as `<rect>` or `<path>` with `fill="currentColor" fill-opacity="0.35" stroke="none"`; `data-icon-trailer-cargo` on the trailer's inner rectangle, drawn at `TRAILER_CARGO` with `fill="none"` (V5 draws the bars inside it).
   - the aria-label escaped for an attribute (`&`, `<`, `>`, `"` and `'`), by the module's own small escaper since it imports nothing.
   - legible at 28 px and handsome at 160 px: what must read at 28 px is the silhouette (the carton, the pouch, the fire, the pallet, the truck, the phone, the hands); the words and fine lines are for the larger sizes. Each icon's string under 5,000 bytes; the module under 60 KB.

- The figures simplified where 28 px demands it, as law 2 allows; each simplification named in the report.
- The words in the five icons stay as drawn (English labels inside pictures, like CRYO and COA); the aria-labels are the panes' to translate.
