## The law to build

1. THE RIGHT NOW PANE, `data-hc-trim-rightnow`, first inside the Trimming section, above the two shift cards and the width of the section: a head reading "Right now" with the farm's today and the shift on duty by the clock (the Day shift or the Second Shift by the shift windows, "no shift on duty" in a gap between them), then the machine picture for the live line, then the BTR bar under it. It is shift and date agnostic: it reads the same whatever day the control picks. THE LIVE FIGURES: today's packages at the trim node (the farm's today, from the start hour to now), every operator counted, the two shifts and any operator on neither shift together; today's lots finished on the line with their WIP left; the packages of the last 30 minutes; the queue. The picture's endpoints carry those figures (TRIM, BIO, BUD with bulk and prepack under it, T-0 with the grams weighed and the package count, and under the picture today's line total, weighed plus WIP left, in place of the shift total); the T-0's typed input line is not drawn here (the inputs are the shift cards'). The picture fills the pane's width (the `.hc-trimline` max-width lifted inside this pane), so the pane is the machine layout "sized to the width of everything else".
2. THE BTR BAR, `data-hc-btr-bar`, under the picture, the pane's full width: one horizontal bar whose filled length is today's live BTR (Total Bud over today's line total, the arithmetic of a shift's BTR on the live figures), the waffle's bud color for the filled part and its consumed color for the rest, the percent as text at its end ("BTR 61.2%"), the bogey (app_config `trim_bogey_pct`, canon 0.30.249) as a tick on the bar with its percent under the tick; when nothing has run today the bar is an empty outline reading "no packages today". A pure function, `btrBar(btr, bogeyPct, locale)`, tested.
3. RUNNING AND DOWN on the pane's head. RUNNING (`.hc-running`, as today) when any package was weighed at the trim node in the last 30 minutes or a shift is on duty with a lot standing in the Unloaded Queue for trim (the law of canon 0.30.163, the two shifts together). Otherwise DOWN, `data-hc-trim-down`, bold in the danger color, with the timer beside it counting from the instant of the last package weighed at the trim node, any day: a `[data-clock]` span with `data-state="running"` and `data-started` at that instant's ms, its first text rendered on the server, the strip's clock script ticking it after; when no package was ever weighed the pane reads DOWN with no timer. The picture's flow bars move while RUNNING and stand still while DOWN, as they do today on a card.
4. THE SHIFT CARDS keep the day picked and lose their machine picture: `.hc-visuals` holds the bud column alone, centered (the waffle, the BTR read, the legend); the head keeps the shift's name, its operators, the day and its own RUNNING flag; the metrics list keeps Other, Bulk Bud, Prepack Bud, Total Bud, BTR, Lbs Produced Yesterday, DLH Yesterday with its hours box, and Lbs/DLH; the input lbs box stays. The line "Cutting and bucking hours from the sheet" leaves the cards (the doc's item 3); its sheet sync status line moves under the Total pane's hours (law 6). `trimLinePicture` serves the Right now pane and is not drawn on a card.
5. THE DAY'S TOTALS, `data-hc-trim-totals`, under the two cards, one line for the day picked, shift agnostic: Total Lbs (every package of the day at the trim node, the two shifts and any operator on neither, in pounds with the grams under), Total Hours (the two shifts' typed DLH summed, the typed one alone when only one is typed, "type hours" when neither), Lbs/DLH (Total Lbs over Total Hours; nothing without hours). The notes for the unassigned grams and the unassigned RUNNING stay under it.
