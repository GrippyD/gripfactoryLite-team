## The law

1. THE LIST. On Penn North Harvest (the PNN wet weight station) and on Penn South/Arlington Harvest (the field node, ARL and PNS), a "Finished lots" section below the weighing area: that screen's farms' lots whose status is not `weighing` and whose `finished_at` falls in the farm's today or the 7 farm days before it (the farm-day bounds of `farmDayBounds`), newest finish first. Each row: the HBN's last five and strain, the lot's space, the finish time (Eastern), the tote count and the wet total (lb, with grams), the status in words. Folded by default (a `details`), so the weighing area stays first.
2. THE LOT. Tapping a row opens the lot on its own page (or folds it open in place): every tote of the lot, newest first, with its time (Eastern), its weight in grams and lb, its field load where it has one, and who weighed it; voided totes stay listed, struck through, with when, who and the reason. The lot's live total and count at the top, equal to the sum of its live totes.
3. VOID A TOTE. A Void button on each live tote, a required reason, the app's actor; the tote gets `deleted_at` and `deleted_by` (soft, never removed), a `record_change_log` row carries the reason, and in the same batch the lot's `weight_grams` and the lot's minted wet package (`local_inventory_items` of that lot, SPR or WIP, its `qty`) drop by the tote's grams. Allowed while the lot is `weighing`, `in_transit` or `drying` (its wet package is still the wet weight); refused once the lot is `unloaded`, `trimmed` or `frozen`, in words that say the wet weight is closed and to ask the Scribe for a repair. The same refusal when the package has children or has left its space.
4. REOPEN. A Reopen button on a `drying` or `in_transit` lot (not on unloaded, trimmed or frozen) does what the queue's Demote does to a lot today (status back to `weighing` through the same code path, the finish stamp cleared, the field close cleared, logged), so nothing about reopening is new law; the button only puts it where the floor weighs. The reopened lot opens on its weigh screen with ALL its live totes listed and counted (not only a new load's), and a new tote adds to the same lot. A reopened lot's minted package is handled exactly as the Demote handles it today; the report says what that is.
5. THE GATE. Void and Reopen sit behind the same gate the queue's Demote has (the Admin PIN where Demote has it); the list and the lot page are open to the floor.
6. THE LIST'S EFFECT ON THE HBN LISTS: none. Listing a finished lot does not bring its HBN back onto the HBN Queue or the field select; only a Reopen does, by the existing law.
7. WORDS: EN and ES for every label, button, help line and refusal.
