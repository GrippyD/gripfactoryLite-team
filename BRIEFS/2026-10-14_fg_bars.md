## The bar, per category (the owner's rules)
Two kinds:
- MIX bars fill the whole track; segments sized by their share of the total.
- CAP bars are one segment, width total over cap (never past 100 percent). The label line reads "TOTAL of CAP UNIT". Over the cap the segment turns the theme's danger color and a red note sits right of the bar.

| Row | Kind | Segments or cap | Over the cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| BUD | MIX | by the listing's THC: 19.99% or less, 20 to 25%, 25 to 30%, 30% and up (20.00 to 24.99 is 20 to 25; 25.00 to 29.99 is 25 to 30), plus a grey "no THC" segment when any bud has none; weighted by pounds | none |
| TRI | CAP | 40,000 lb (app pounds, 448 g) | at 40,000 lb and above: red, note "N trucks", N = round(total lb / 8,000) |
| SHA | CAP | 5,000 lb | above 5,000 lb: red, note is the total weight |
| PRE | MIX | by strain, weight share | none |
| Distillate | CAP | 2,000 L | above 2,000 L: red, note is the total liters |
| Isolate | CAP | 68,000 g | above 68,000 g: red, note is the total grams |
| Infused Bud | CAP | 20,000 g | above 20,000 g: red, note is the total grams |
| Prerolls | MIX | each by flavor; segment text "FLAVOR: N" | none |
| Vapes | MIX | each by hardware type (1g, 2g, ...); segment text "SIZE: N, K flavors" (count of flavors, not their names) | none |

Segment text: inside the segment, ellipsis when it does not fit, and a `title` with the full name and amount. Text stays readable on every fill (dark text on light fills, light on dark). MIX colors: one ordered palette from theme tokens; the BUD bands run light to dark with THC. An empty category shows an empty track. The bar carries `role="img"` and an `aria-label` that reads the segments and amounts. Data attributes for tests: `data-fg-bar="KEY"`, each segment `data-fg-seg="NAME"` and `data-fg-share`, over cap `data-fg-over="1"`, the truck note `data-fg-trucks="N"`.

Caps and the truck size live as named constants in `src/fginventory.ts` (`FG_CAPS`, `FG_TRUCK_LB = 8000`), EN and ES words in i18n ("trucks" / "camiones", "of" / "de", "no THC" / "sin THC").
