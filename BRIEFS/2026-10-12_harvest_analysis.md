## Why

The owner wants harvest analysis by strain where the Fulfillment people can reach it, and a forecast of dry pounds that is smarter than a mean. Today the console's Assumed Dry and Estimated Bud (canon 0.30.356, `src/console.ts` around lines 206 to 930) apply one farm-wide average dry-down and one season BTR to everything still drying. Strains do not dry or trim alike, and the dry-down drifts through the season, so the farm average is wrong in both directions. The 10/13 meeting wants this pressure-tested: so every number the page shows must say how it was made.

## The law

1. THE PLACE. A new tile "Harvest Analysis" on the Fulfillment menu, to a page `/fulfillment/harvest-analysis` (EN and ES words in `src/i18n.ts`) with three panes chosen by `?pane=`: `dry` (Per Strain Dry), `wet` (Per Strain Wet), `btr` (Per Strain BTR), a pane switch at the top like the other pane pages. Filters shared by all three: Harvest Year (default the current), Farm (blank all, PNN, PNS, ARL), Grow Method (blank all, indoor, outdoor, greenhouse). Read only. Same access as the Fulfillment menu.
2. THE GRAIN. One row per strain, the strain named by the rule above (the market strain when one is typed, else the real strain; one name per row, lots of the same name pooled across farms unless a Farm is chosen). A Total row beneath.
3. PER STRAIN WET (the `wet` pane). Lots, wet lb (house pound, two decimals), average wet lb per lot, plants when the lots carry a plant count and wet lb per plant, the share of the year's wet. Lots with no wet weight are not counted.
4. PER STRAIN DRY (the `dry` pane): the actual and the forecast. Per strain: lots harvested, lots unloaded, wet lb of the unloaded lots, dry lb of the unloaded lots, the dry-down percent (dry over wet) of the unloaded lots pooled by weight (the weighted mean, shown as "Mean"), the forecast dry-down percent (law 5), lots still drying and their wet lb, the forecast dry lb of those lots (wet times the forecast dry-down), Assumed Dry lb (actual dry plus forecast dry), and, beside it, Assumed Dry lb at the mean, so the difference between the two methods is on the page. Frozen and fresh frozen lots stay out, as in V22; if V22 shows them as a separate line, show the same line.
5. THE FORECAST METHOD, and it is stated on the page in one plain paragraph. For each strain, the unloaded lots ordered by unload date give a series of dry-down percents (dry over wet, per lot, not pooled). With four or more lots, the forecast is the next point of the least squares line through the series (x the lot's order, y the percent), clamped to between 5 and 60 percent and never more than 10 points away from the strain's weighted mean; the page shows the slope in points per lot, the number of lots, and the spread (one standard deviation of the residuals). With fewer than four lots the forecast is the weighted mean and the row is flagged "Mean, fewer than 4 lots". When a strain has no unloaded lot at all, the forecast is the farm's pooled dry-down (the V22 rate) and the row is flagged "Farm rate". Never a blank forecast, never a division by zero. The trend line (law 8) draws the same fit.
6. PER STRAIN BTR (the `btr` pane). Per strain: lots trimmed, weight ran, BUD (the numerator of BTR exactly as the trimming code in V22 computes it: the canon defines BTR as (BUD+WPR+SHA) over all weight ran; read that code and match it, do not redefine it), BTR percent (pooled by weight), the forecast BTR percent by the same method as law 5 over the strain's trimmed lots ordered by trim finish, and the flag rules of law 5. Then the Estimated Bud: the bud already trimmed plus the forecast BTR applied to the strain's forecast dry lb still untrimmed (dry or drying, not yet trimmed), shown as a range for shrink: Low and High, where shrink is the loss between the estimate and what is packaged, read from two settings.
7. THE SHRINK SETTINGS. `app_config` keys `bud_shrink_low_pct` (default 2) and `bud_shrink_high_pct` (default 5), read through `getConfigValue` with those fallbacks when the key is absent or not a number from 0 to 50; no migration. Estimated Bud Low is the estimate times one minus the high shrink, High is the estimate times one minus the low shrink. The page says how the range is made. Do not change the console's Assumed Dry or Estimated Bud boxes in this brief: they stay on the farm average until the owner has pressure-tested this page (a later brief moves them).
8. THE TREND LINE. In the dry and btr panes, each strain row opens (a details element, no script needed) to an inline SVG: the series as points, the fitted line, the forecast as a marked point at the next position, the mean as a dashed line. EN and ES labels; both themes (use the theme tokens in `src/theme.ts`, no new colors). One SVG per strain row, drawn only when the row is opened if you can do it without script (a details element renders its children anyway, so cap it: draw the SVG only for strains with four or more lots and at most 60 strains).
9. THE CSV on each pane, on the standing control (the same `format=csv` pattern as HR1): one row per strain, every column above, percents with two decimals, pounds with two decimals, no thousands commas.
10. THE FIELD ESTIMATE (FE1, live). In the dry pane, two more columns per strain: Field Estimate (dry lb) and Forecast less Estimate (dry lb). The estimate is the sum of `estimatesAsOf(db, the farm's today)` rows whose strain names the row (the same naming rule as law 2, applied to the estimate's `strain_id`) and whose farm is the chosen Farm, or every farm when Farm is blank. Forecast is the row's actual dry plus forecast dry. Blank, not zero, when the strain has no estimate in force. Estimates whose strain has no row this year are summed in one line under the Total ("Field estimates with no harvest yet: n strains, x lb"). The CSV carries both columns. Stored pounds are used as typed, no conversion.
11. NOTHING ELSE MOVES. The console boxes, the existing reports, the feeds, the cron blocks, the Fulfillment pages already there stay as they are. No migration, no new table, no cron job.
