## What the Scribe read in the code

THE MESS, read from those facts and his words: when a harvest batch fills more than one truck, or one truck carries the end of one HBN and the start of the next, the crew has one lot per HBN per truck to start and finish by hand, and an HBN leaves the pick pane the moment one of its lots is finished with no weighing lot left (`NO_FINISHED_LOT_SQL`, the 2026-08-28 ruling), so the next truck of the same HBN cannot be started unless it was started before the last one finished.

WHAT STANDS DOWNSTREAM, one lot one HBN: the Harvest CSV (`PENDING_WET_UPLOADS_SQL`, `wetWeightsCsv*`, one HarvestName per lot), the born package (`getInventoryItemForLot`, one item per lot, `shortLotId` as its code), the trim line and its packages (`wip_packages.wet_weight_entry_id`, the lot's batch), the cryo pools by HBN, the unload session and its files per lot, the tiles (`listSiloLotGrams`, `loadSiloVisuals`), the reports, the console's Plants laws, the test orders, the cure panes, THE HBN THAT CAME BACK pencil (the whole lot moves while weighing). LOAD SILO AND UNLOAD: Load Silo (`src/drying.ts`, the `/station/load_silo` routes in `src/index.ts`, `loadSilo` in `src/actions.ts`) lists the drying and in_transit lots with no open load (`listBarnWetLots`), preselects the HBN's planned silo, writes one `silo_loads` row per lot (the clock's zero `loaded_at`, Start and Pause), one open load per lot, one per space when `spaceHoldsOneLot` (space_type silo), several on a dry room or freezer; Unload Dry Space is the direct ledger station over `dry_weights` with `unload_sessions` per lot, `finishUnload` and its three modes (`planUnloadFinish`), the reconcile gate and the package file pends. ## PART ONE: THE TRUCK RECORD

1. THE TRUCK ON THE HEADER. The wet weight header form regains `Truck #` (`name="truck_number"`, free text, optional): prefilled with the next number among the farm's lots of the same farm day (`effective_date`, else the created day in farm time; `nextTruckNumber` over their distinct truck numbers), the crew may overtype or blank it. A blank Truck # is a lot that goes straight to its space (PNN's own harvest). `createWetWeight` stores it; THE FOUR START TAPS guard keeps matching on it. Move To stays exactly THE BLANK SLATE's (a PNN silo or hang room); the truck sentinel and `truck-<n>` stay refused there. The lots in progress list, the lot panel's meta line, the Load Silo labels and the Dry Space Queue's grouping read `Truck <n> - <hbn>` from `truck_number` where the code already carries that branch (`truck_label`, `barnLotLabel`), a lot without a truck reading as today.

2. THE HBN LINE ON EVERY TOTE (his words). Migration 0075: `ALTER TABLE wet_weights ADD COLUMN harvest_batch_id TEXT` (null reads as the lot's own HBN, so every existing tote keeps its HBN without a backfill) and an index on it. The weights form on the lot view gains a select `name="harvest_batch_id"` beside the grams: its options are the minted HBNs, meaning every undeleted `harvest_batches` row of the header's farm whose status is open (recalled and complete excluded), the header's HBN first and selected until another is chosen, then the rest newest first by entry_date then created_at, each labeled as the pick pane labels an HBN; the selected option is the HBN of the newest live tote of this lot (the lot's own HBN when there is none), so the last one selected is the default for the next tote and stays until the crew changes it. `addLotWeight` stores the chosen id (refusing an id that is not on that list, in words), logs it with the row's other fields, and the recompute is unchanged (the lot's live total counts every tote whatever its HBN). Each tote row on the ledger table shows its HBN's serial after the grams (the whole HBN in the title) when it differs from the header's, and the panel's meta line gains `HBNs on this lot: 26041 12 totes 1,234 g · 26042 3 totes 310 g` when more than one HBN rides it. The tote pencil (`EDITABLE_FIELDS.wet_weights`) gains `harvest_batch_id` (the same list, a reason, refused once the lot is not weighing, the way THE HBN THAT CAME BACK refuses); the lot pencil's HBN moves the header HBN and every tote that carries no explicit HBN or the old header HBN, as today.

3. THE PANE KEEPS TODAY'S HBN. `NO_FINISHED_LOT_SQL` learns the farm day: a batch hides only when every one of its finished lots finished before today (farm time, `effective_date` else the finishing day) and it has no weighing lot; an HBN finished today stays on the pick pane for its next truck. The 2026-08-28 ruling's purpose (yesterday's HBN never comes back by itself) holds.

4. THE FINISH SPLITS THE TRUCK INTO ONE LOT PER HBN. `finishLot` on a lot whose live totes carry more than one HBN: the lot keeps the header HBN and the totes that carry it (or no explicit HBN); for each other HBN, in one `db.batch`, a new `wet_weight_entries` row is minted as a copy of the header (farm day and `effective_date`, `actual_dryspc`, `truck_number`, `material_form`, `actual_start_time`, `team_lead`, `lead_person_id`, `notes`, the actor) under that HBN, its totes moved to it (`UPDATE wet_weights SET wet_weight_entry_id` per row, one log row each under `SYSTEM_REASON.truck_split`, a new reason), every lot's total recomputed, every lot finished to drying with the same transition and log the finish writes today, and one harvest package minted per lot by `planFinishMint` as today (kind from the material form, PNN's BARN, the landing license), so one package per HBN is born and every downstream reader sees ordinary lots. The finish form asks the plant count and the METRC tag per HBN on the lot: the plant prompt lists every HBN riding the lot with one `plant_count[<hbnId>]` box each, and the tag input becomes one `metrc_tag[<hbnId>]` per HBN (the single-HBN lot keeps today's single box and field names); each lot takes its own count and tag. The package code: `shortLotId(hbn)` as today, with `-T<n>` appended when the lot carries a truck number (`26041-T3`), so two trucks of one HBN never share a code. The flash names every lot born of the split with its HBN, tote count and grams (`Finished Truck 3: 26041 12 totes 1,234 g; 26042 3 totes 310 g.`), Spanish too. A truck whose totes all carry the header's HBN finishes exactly as today. The split lots ride on the Harvest CSV as separate lots of their own HBNs (the file per HBN is unchanged in shape), and every unload, trim, cryo, testing and report reader is untouched.

5. THE WORDS. Every new string with Spanish: the Truck # label and its help, the HBN select's label, the meta line, the refusals, the flash, the pencil's field.

6. THE TESTS, PART ONE. `test/start_taps.test.ts` and `test/hbn_pencil.test.ts` extended; a new `test/truck.test.ts`: the header's next truck number per farm day; a lot with three HBNs' totes (the default following the last selection; the list order; a foreign HBN refused); the finish split (three lots on one truck number, the totes moved with log rows, three packages with `-T<n>` codes, the plant counts and tags per HBN, the flash); a single-HBN lot finishing as before; the pane keeping a batch finished today and hiding one finished yesterday; the tote pencil refused on a finished lot; the Harvest CSV per split lot. `scripts/smoke.mjs`: a truck at ARL with two HBNs' totes finished, the two lots on Load Silo's pane under `Truck 1 - <hbn>`, both packages in the barn, both loadable to different silos.

## PART TWO: THE DRYING SCHEDULE

7. THREE SURFACES BY SPACE KIND (his pick). `SCHEDULE_STAGES` gains `silos`, `dry_rooms` and `freezers` (titles Silos, Dry Rooms, Freezers) under `PRODUCTION_SCHEDULING_PATH/<stage>`, each the `.station-triple` of the lines: AVAILABLE lists every lot Load Silo lists today (`listBarnWetLots`: drying, no open load) grouped by HBN with its truck, its wet grams and its planned space, and `>>` on a lot opens Load Silo's picked form with the space select limited to this kind's spaces and the planned space preselected when it is of this kind (a lot may go to any space of any kind; that is his "shares to any space", one lot per HBN share after part one); WORKING lists the open loads of this kind's spaces grouped under each space (the lot's label, the clock as the tiles read it, the wet grams, Start and Pause as Load Silo's, and `>` Unload opening Unload Dry Space picked on the lot); COMPLETE lists this kind's lots unloaded in the last seven days (the dry grams, the yield percent, the unload's day). No new table: the three surfaces are views over `wet_weight_entries`, `silo_loads`, `unload_sessions` and the spaces (`space_type`, `spaceTypeOf`); no `production_schedules` row is written for them. The Drying Operations landing gains the three tiles; Load Silo and Unload Dry Space stay as the acts' forms; the console tiles read the same rows as before.

8. THE JOINT UNLOAD, DRY PRO RATA (his pick). When a dry room or freezer, or a silo that holds several open loads, is unloaded as a whole, the Working pane's space header carries `Unload together`: one form asking the total dry grams and the unload's questions once (the operator, the material questions Unload Dry Space asks, the reconcile choice the gate offers since every dry total falls short of the wet-born grams, the effective day), and the app writes each open load's share as that lot's single `dry_weights` row, the share being the total dry times the lot's wet grams over the wet grams of every load in the group (whole grams, round half up, the last share taking the remainder so the shares sum to the total), then runs each lot's unload finish exactly as `finishUnload` does today with that one choice (the same modes, the same file pends), all under one reason naming the group. A space with one open load has no such control. The freezer branch of `finishUnload` (a line removed, nothing weighed) is unchanged and needs no joint form.

9. NOTHING ELSE MOVES. The trim, pasteurization and prepack surfaces and THE UNFINISH; the tiles' fill laws; the Harvest CSV and the unload files; the mirror; the sheet sync; the repair scripts; the unrouted road and arrivals code (leave it as it stands).

10. THE TESTS, PART TWO. A new `test/dryingschedule.test.ts`: each of the three surfaces rendering its panes from fixtures (a lot waiting with a planned silo on Silos and not on Dry Rooms unless it is planned there, an open load under its space with the clock, an unloaded lot on Complete with its yield), the `>>` link carrying the kind and the preselection, the joint unload's shares summing to the total with the remainder on the last, and its refusal on a space with one load; `test/scheduling.test.ts` for the landing tiles. `scripts/smoke.mjs`: the three surfaces answering 200 with their panes, the two split lots of part one loaded to two silos from the Silos surface, a dry room with two loads unloaded together and both lots reading unloaded with shares summing to the typed total.
